# Project UIL Constitution

The **Constitution of Project UIL** is the highest governing document of the initiative.

It establishes the foundational identity, mission, principles, governance, ethics, and long-term direction of Project UIL. Every policy, standard, methodology, publication, and decision within the project should remain consistent with the principles defined in this document.

The Constitution exists to preserve the integrity of Project UIL as it evolves over time, ensuring that its purpose remains unchanged regardless of future growth, contributors, technologies, or organizational changes.

---

# Purpose

The purpose of the Constitution is to:

- Define the identity and purpose of Project UIL.
- Establish the project's mission and long-term vision.
- Protect the principles that guide all research and decisions.
- Provide a governance framework for contributors and future stewardship.
- Preserve the integrity, independence, and continuity of the initiative.

The Constitution serves as the permanent foundation upon which every other Project UIL document is built.

# Authority

The Constitution is the highest governing document within Project UIL.

All other documentation—including standards, methodologies, policies, manuals, and operational procedures—should align with and support the principles established in this Constitution.

If any future document conflicts with the Constitution, the Constitution takes precedence.

---

# Usage

This document should be referenced whenever:

- Establishing project direction.
- Making significant strategic decisions.
- Defining research policies.
- Evaluating collaborations and partnerships.
- Resolving governance questions.
- Reviewing ethical considerations.
- Creating or revising official Project UIL documentation.

Every major decision should be consistent with the principles established by the Constitution.

---

# Guiding Philosophy

> **Meaningful technology begins with meaningful understanding.**

> **Observation before invention. Understanding before implementation. Research before solutions.**

> **Every decision should strengthen the mission of understanding human problems before building technology.**
