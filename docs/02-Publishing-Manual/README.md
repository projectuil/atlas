# Project UIL Publishing Manual

The **Publishing Manual** defines the official standards for creating, reviewing, and publishing content under the Project UIL brand.

Its purpose is to ensure that every public communication reflects the project's mission, research philosophy, and commitment to evidence-based thinking. Whether publishing a social media post, research article, presentation, or community response, these guidelines help maintain consistency, credibility, and long-term trust.

---

# Purpose

The Publishing Manual establishes a standardized publishing process by defining:

- Publishing philosophy
- Content categories
- Writing standards
- Review workflow
- Research ethics
- Community engagement
- Long-term publishing goals

Every piece of published content should align with the mission and values of Project UIL.

# Usage

This folder should be referenced whenever creating:

- Social media posts
- Research updates
- Atlas observations
- Articles and blog posts
- Presentations
- Public announcements
- Community discussions
- Any other public communication representing Project UIL

Following these guidelines ensures that every publication remains consistent with the Project UIL brand and research philosophy.

---

# Guiding Philosophy

> **Understand human problems before building technology.**

> **Publishing should document research, not chase attention.**

> **Every publication should leave the reader with a clearer understanding of a human problem or a better question to explore.**
