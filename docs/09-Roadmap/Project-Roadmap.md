# Project UIL Roadmap

**Version:** 1.0  
**Timeframe:** 2026–2031

---

# Purpose

This roadmap outlines the long-term strategic direction of Project UIL over the next five years.

It defines the major phases of growth, research priorities, and long-term objectives that will guide the evolution of Project UIL from an independent research initiative into a mature body of knowledge capable of informing future technologies.

The roadmap serves as a strategic reference rather than a fixed timeline. As Project UIL evolves, milestones may be refined while remaining consistent with the project's Constitution and guiding principles.

---

# Vision

Project UIL seeks to contribute to a future where technology is designed from a deep understanding of human behaviour, cognition, and interaction.

Rather than beginning with products, Project UIL begins with observation, research, and structured knowledge.

---

# Strategic Roadmap

## Phase I — Foundation

**Timeframe:** 2026

### Objective

Establish the foundations of Project UIL.

### Key Milestones

- Establish the Project UIL identity.
- Publish governance documentation.
- Define research methodology.
- Develop the ATLAS repository.
- Create Human Friction classification standards.
- Build documentation and repository structures.
- Begin documenting Human Frictions.

### Primary Deliverables

- Constitution
- Brand Book
- Research Standards
- Publishing Documentation
- ATLAS Repository
- Initial Human Frictions

---

## Phase II — Research Expansion

**Timeframe:** 2027

### Objective

Expand ATLAS into a comprehensive repository of Human Frictions.

### Key Milestones

- Continue documenting Human Frictions.
- Expand research categories.
- Improve validation processes.
- Establish research patterns.
- Publish observation-driven research.
- Build a growing public knowledge base.

### Primary Deliverables

- Hundreds of validated Human Frictions
- Pattern Repository
- Research publications
- Community discussions
- Improved research workflows

---

## Phase III — Knowledge Development

**Timeframe:** 2028

### Objective

Transform observations into structured knowledge.

### Key Milestones

- Discover recurring behavioural patterns.
- Publish Research Insights.
- Develop conceptual frameworks.
- Strengthen systems thinking research.
- Expand interdisciplinary research.

### Primary Deliverables

- Pattern Library
- Research Insights
- Behavioural Frameworks
- Systems Thinking Models

## Phase IV — Architecture

## Phase V — Innovation

**Timeframe:** 2030–2031

### Objective

Transform research into practical innovation.

# Continuous Objectives

Throughout every phase, Project UIL will continue to:

- Observe Human Frictions.
- Expand ATLAS.
- Improve research quality.
- Encourage thoughtful discussion.
- Strengthen documentation.
- Preserve research integrity.
- Build public trust.
- Support accessibility.
- Promote human-centred thinking.

---

# Research Evolution

Project UIL follows a structured progression of knowledge.

```
Real Human Experiences
        ↓
Observed Human Frictions
        ↓
Validated Human Frictions
        ↓
Patterns
        ↓
Research Insights
        ↓
Architectural Principles
        ↓
Reference Architectures
        ↓
Future Technologies
```

Knowledge should always evolve in this direction.

Project UIL does not begin with products.

It begins with understanding.

# Strategic Priorities

The long-term priorities of Project UIL are:

1. Human Understanding
2. Research Quality
3. Knowledge Organization
4. Evidence-Based Thinking
5. Accessibility
6. Ethical Research
7. Long-Term Value
8. Continuous Improvement

---

# Success Indicators

Progress should be evaluated through indicators such as:

- Growth of the ATLAS repository.
- Quality of documented Human Frictions.
- Discovery of meaningful Patterns.
- Development of Research Insights.
- Consistency of research methodology.
- Community engagement and collaboration.
- Contributions to human-centred thinking.
- Long-term usefulness of published knowledge.

Success is measured by the quality of understanding created—not by popularity.

---

# Guiding Principle

Project UIL exists to deepen humanity's understanding of how people interact with the world before designing technologies intended to improve it.

Every phase of this roadmap should strengthen that mission.
