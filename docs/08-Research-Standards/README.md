# Project UIL Research Standards & ATLAS Methodology

The **Research Standards & ATLAS Methodology** define the official research process used by Project UIL to discover, validate, document, maintain, and evolve Human Frictions within the ATLAS Research Repository.

This document establishes the principles, standards, workflows, and governance that ensure every Human Friction is researched consistently, documented objectively, and preserved as reliable knowledge.

Every observation accepted into ATLAS should follow the methodology described in this document.

---

# Purpose

The purpose of the Research Standards & ATLAS Methodology is to:

- Establish a consistent research methodology.
- Define the standards for documenting Human Frictions.
- Maintain the quality and integrity of the ATLAS repository.
- Ensure research remains evidence-based, reproducible, and human-centred.
- Provide a scalable framework for future researchers and contributors.

This methodology serves as the operational foundation of Project UIL research.

# Usage

This folder should be referenced whenever:

- Observing Human Frictions.
- Validating research findings.
- Creating new ATLAS entries.
- Assigning APIDs.
- Reviewing or updating observations.
- Forming research patterns.
- Maintaining the ATLAS repository.
- Developing Project UIL research methodologies.

Following these standards ensures that every contribution to ATLAS is consistent, traceable, and aligned with the research philosophy of Project UIL.

---

# Guiding Philosophy

> **Technology should begin with understanding, not assumptions.**

> **Observe honestly. Validate carefully. Document clearly. Build responsibly.**

> **Solutions are the outcome of disciplined research—not the starting point.**
