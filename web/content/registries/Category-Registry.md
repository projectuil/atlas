<p><strong>Category Registry</strong></p><p><strong>Purpose</strong></p><p>The Category Registry serves as the authoritative master index of all Human Friction Categories defined within the ATLAS taxonomy. It establishes the official Category Identifiers (Category IDs), APID prefixes, category names, and their primary research domains, ensuring every Human Friction is consistently classified throughout the repository.</p><p>The Category Registry functions as the single source of truth for category definitions and APID prefix assignments.</p><p><strong>Registry Friction Category shall receive a unique Category ID before being added to the ATLAS taxonomy.</li><li>Every category shall own a unique APID prefix.</li><li>Human Frictions shall use only the APID prefix assigned to their category.</li><li>New categories shall receive a new Category ID and APID prefix before Human Frictions are documented.</li><li>Category IDs and APID prefixes are permanent identifiers and shall never be reused.</li><li>Each Human Friction shall belong to one primary category.</li><li>The Category Registry serves as the authoritative reference for category definitions and APID prefix assignments.</li><li>Changes to category names, domains, or classifications shall be reflected in this Registry.</li><li>Category statistics shall be updated whenever Human Frictions are added, archived, or reclassified.</li></ol><p><strong>Registry
