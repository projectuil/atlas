<p><strong>Pattern Registry</strong></p><p><strong>Purpose</strong></p><p>The Pattern Registry serves as the authoritative master index of all Patterns documented within the ATLAS repository. It provides a centralized reference for tracking every Pattern Identifier (PTID), ensuring each Pattern can be uniquely identified, referenced, and maintained throughout its lifecycle.</p><p>The Registry functions as the single source of truth for Pattern identification. Detailed information-including supporting APIDs, evidence, research notes, validation confidence, revision history, and document metadata-is maintained within the corresponding Pattern Rules</strong></p><ol><li>Every Pattern shall receive a unique PTID before being added to the ATLAS repository.</li><li>Each PTID shall appear only once within the Registry.</li><li>The Registry shall be updated whenever a Pattern is created, accepted, revised, or deprecated.</li><li>PTIDs are permanent identifiers and shall never be reused.</li><li>Detailed information-including supporting APIDs, evidence, research notes, validation confidence, and revision history-shall be maintained within the corresponding Pattern document.</li><li>The Pattern Registry serves solely as the authoritative index for identifying and locating Patterns.</li><li>Patterns shall be listed in ascending PTID order.</li><li>Every Pattern shall reference one or more supporting Human Frictions (APIDs) within its corresponding Pattern document.</li><li>Changes to a Pattern's title or status shall be reflected in this Registry.</li></ol><p><strong>Document
