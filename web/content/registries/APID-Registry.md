<p><strong>APID Registry</strong></p><p><strong>Purpose</strong></p><p>The APID Registry serves as the authoritative master index of all Human Frictions documented within the ATLAS repository. It provides a centralized reference for tracking every Human Friction using a unique APID (ATLAS Problem Identifier), ensuring each documented observation can be consistently referenced, maintained, and expanded throughout its lifecycle.</p><p>The Registry functions as the single source of truth for Human Friction identification. Detailed metadata, evidence, research notes, revision history, and supporting information are maintained within the individual Human Friction documents rather than duplicated in this Registry.</p><p><strong>Registry
