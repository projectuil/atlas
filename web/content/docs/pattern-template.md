__1\. Purpose__

The Pattern Template defines the official structure for documenting Patterns within the ATLAS Research Repository\.

A Pattern represents a recurring behaviour, relationship, or characteristic identified through the analysis of multiple Human Frictions\.

Its purpose is to ensure every Pattern is:

- Evidence\-based 
- Consistent 
- Easy to review 
- Searchable 
- Research\-ready 
- Scalable 

Patterns must always be supported by documented Human Frictions\.

__2\. Core Principle__

A Pattern is __not a single observation__\.

It is a recurring behaviour or relationship identified across multiple Human Frictions\.

Patterns help transform individual observations into reusable research knowledge\.

__4\. Pattern Structure__

Every Pattern should contain the following sections\.

__Pattern__

Describe the recurring behaviour or relationship observed across multiple Human Frictions\.

Focus on what consistently occurs rather than individual examples\.

__Human Impact__

Describe how this Pattern collectively affects people\.

Examples include:

- Increased cognitive load 
- Reduced productivity 
- Decision fatigue 
- Confusion 
- Slower task completion 

__Evidence__

Summarize the Human Frictions that support this Pattern\.

Explain the relationship between them rather than repeating each Human Friction\.

__Research Notes__

Capture information that may strengthen future research\.

Examples include:

- Similar Patterns 
- Related Human Frictions 
- Questions for future investigation 
- Initial hypotheses 
- Connections with other research 

__Revision History__

Maintain a record of significant document changes\.

__6\. Writing Guidelines__

Every Pattern should:

- Be supported by multiple Human Frictions\. 
- Describe one recurring behaviour or relationship\. 
- Use objective language\. 
- Avoid proposing solutions\. 
- Reference supporting APIDs\. 
- Remain concise and easy to review\. 

__7\. Final Principle__

Patterns are the bridge between individual Human Frictions and higher\-level research understanding\.

Each Pattern should capture one recurring behavioural relationship supported by evidence, making ATLAS easier to expand, validate, and understand as the repository grows\.
