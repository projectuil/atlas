__ PROJECT UIL PUBLISHING MANUAL__

__Version 1\.0__

__1\. Purpose__

This manual defines the standards for publishing under the Project UIL brand\.

Every post, article, image, comment, and public interaction must follow these guidelines\.

The objective is to maintain consistency, credibility, and trust over the long term\.

__2\. Publishing Mission__

Every publication must help achieve the mission:

__Understand human problems before building technology\.__

If a post does not support this mission, it should not be published\.

__3\. Publishing Philosophy__

Project UIL exists to document research-not to chase attention\.

Publishing is a way to:

- Document observations 
- Share research 
- Encourage thoughtful discussion 
- Learn from others 
- Improve understanding 

Publishing is __not__ for:

- Going viral 
- Self\-promotion 
- Showing expertise for its own sake 
- Following trends 

__4\. Content Categories__

Every post must belong to __one__ category\.

__Code__

__Category__

__Purpose__

AO

Atlas Observation

Share one Human Friction

RP

Research Pattern

Connect multiple observations

RJ

Research Journal

Weekly progress and reflections

HS

Human Story

Real\-world experience that reveals a friction

ST

Systems Thinking

Explain broader systems or interactions

OQ

Open Question

Invite thoughtful discussion

RM

Research Milestone

Share meaningful project progress

Never publish a post that doesn't clearly fit one of these\.

__5\. Publishing Rules__

Every post must:

- Be based on observation, research, or a clearly stated question\. 
- Be honest about what is known and what is still uncertain\. 
- Respect people's privacy\. 
- Avoid sensational language\. 
- End with a purpose \(insight, reflection, or question\)\. 

__6\. Writing Style__

Every post should follow this structure:

__1\. Hook__

One sentence that introduces the observation or idea\.

__2\. Context__

Explain what was observed\.

__3\. Insight__

Why does this matter?

__4\. Reflection__

What does this reveal about people or systems?

__5\. Closing__

A thoughtful question or concluding insight\.

__7\. Tone of Voice__

Every piece of writing should be:

- Calm 
- Curious 
- Respectful 
- Precise 
- Thoughtful 
- Accessible 

Avoid:

- Clickbait 
- Hype 
- Mockery 
- Overconfidence 
- Excessive emojis 
- ALL CAPS for emphasis 

__8\. Image Standards__

Every image should follow the Brand Book\.

Requirements:

- Matte black background 
- White typography 
- Emerald green accent 
- Minimal layout 
- Consistent fonts 
- Plenty of whitespace 

Do not use:

- AI clichs 
- Busy graphics 
- Decorative effects that distract from the message 

__9\. Quality Checklist__

Before publishing, ask:

- Is the observation genuine? 
- Is the wording clear? 
- Does it align with the mission? 
- Is the information accurate? 
- Is the image consistent with the brand? 
- Does the post encourage thoughtful discussion? 

If any answer is "No", revise before publishing\.

__10\. Research Ethics__

Project UIL will:

- Distinguish observations from hypotheses\. 
- Credit external ideas and sources where used\. 
- Respect privacy when describing real experiences\. 
- Avoid presenting assumptions as facts\. 
- Update or correct posts if new evidence changes the understanding\. 

__11\. Comments & Community__

When responding:

- Thank people for thoughtful contributions\. 
- Ask follow\-up questions\. 
- Clarify misunderstandings politely\. 
- Welcome constructive disagreement\. 

Do not:

- Argue to "win"\. 
- Mock or dismiss others\. 
- Engage with abusive comments\. 

__12\. Publishing Frequency__

Aim for __2-3 high\-quality posts per week__\.

Quality always comes before quantity\.

If there is nothing meaningful to share, do not force a post\.

__13\. Content Review Workflow__

Every post follows this process:

Draft

      

Brand Review

      

Visual Design

      

Final Proofreading

      

Publish

      

Community Discussion

      

Archive

__14\. Hashtag Policy__

Use only a few relevant hashtags\.

Examples:

- \#HumanCenteredDesign 
- \#UXResearch 
- \#Accessibility 
- \#SystemsThinking 
- \#Research 
- \#Innovation 

Avoid long lists of unrelated hashtags\.

__15\. What We Never Publish__

Never publish:

- Unverified claims 
- Confidential information 
- Personal attacks 
- Political campaigning 
- Religious campaigning 
- Misleading statistics 
- Fear\-based content 
- AI\-generated facts presented as research 
- Posts written only to attract attention 

__16\. Long\-Term Publishing Goals__

__Year 1__

Build trust through consistent observations\.

__Year 2__

Become recognised for thoughtful research\.

__Year 3__

Publish deeper frameworks and patterns\.

__Year 4__

Introduce research\-backed prototypes or products\.

__17\. Success Metrics__

Rather than measuring only likes or followers, consider:

- Are discussions becoming more thoughtful? 
- Are people sharing their own observations? 
- Are professionals engaging with the research? 
- Is the Atlas knowledge base growing? 
- Is Project UIL becoming associated with observation\-driven thinking? 

These indicators better reflect the project's purpose\.

__18\. Final Publishing Principle__

__Every post should leave the reader with a clearer understanding of a human problem or a better question to explore-not simply another piece of content to scroll past\.__
