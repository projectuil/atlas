__ Project UIL__

__ATLAS Standards__

__Research Paper Template__

__Version 1\.0__

__1\. Purpose__

The Research Paper Template defines the standard structure for documenting Research Papers within the ATLAS Research Repository\.

A Research Paper represents the formal publication of evidence\-based research derived from Human Frictions, Patterns, and Research Insights\.

Its purpose is to ensure that every Research Paper is:

- Evidence\-based 
- Well\-structured 
- Traceable 
- Consistent 
- Suitable for publication 
- Reusable for future research and architecture 

Research Papers are the primary medium through which Project UIL communicates research findings\.

__2\. Core Principle__

__A Research Paper transforms structured research into publishable knowledge by connecting evidence, analysis, and conclusions in a clear and traceable manner\.__

Every claim within a Research Paper should be supported by documented evidence from ATLAS\.

__3\. Field Definitions__

__RPID \(Research Paper Identifier\)__

A permanent and unique identifier assigned to a Research Paper\.

Example:

RP\-001

The identifier remains unchanged throughout the life of the paper\.

__Title__

A clear and descriptive title representing the focus of the paper\.

Examples:

- Understanding Human Frictions in Digital File Management 
- A Taxonomy of Everyday Interaction Frictions 
- Cognitive Load in Modern Digital Workflows 

__Authors__

List the author\(s\) responsible for the paper\.

Example:

Jothish Gandham

__Version__

Current document version\.

Example:

1\.0

__Status__

Current publication stage\.

Allowed values:

- Draft 
- Under Review 
- Published 
- Archived 

__Created__

Creation date\.

Format:

YYYY\-MM\-DD

__Last Updated__

Most recent revision date\.

__Tags__

Keywords describing the research paper\.

Examples:

Human Friction

Research

Navigation

Cognitive Load

Digital Experience

__Supporting Insights__

List every Research Insight supporting the paper\.

Example:

INS\-001

INS\-004

INS\-007

__Supporting Patterns__

List every Pattern discussed\.

Example:

PT\-001

PT\-005

__Supporting APIDs__

Reference the Human Frictions used as evidence\.

Example:

FF\-001

OS\-004

MB\-006

__4\. Research Paper Sections__

__Abstract__

Provide a concise summary of the research\.

Include:

- Purpose 
- Method 
- Findings 
- Conclusion 

__Introduction__

Introduce the research problem\.

Explain why it is important\.

Provide background information\.

__Research Objective__

Clearly define the objective of the paper\.

Example:

To identify recurring Human Frictions in digital file management and understand their impact on user productivity\.

__Research Methodology__

Describe how the research was conducted\.

Include:

- Observation methods 
- Human Friction analysis 
- Pattern identification 
- Insight generation 

__Evidence__

Present the supporting Human Frictions\.

Reference APIDs where appropriate\.

__Pattern Analysis__

Explain the recurring Patterns identified during analysis\.

Reference PTIDs\.

__Research Insights__

Present the validated Research Insights\.

Reference INSIDs\.

__Discussion__

Interpret the findings\.

Discuss:

- Why the Patterns exist 
- Relationships between observations 
- Broader implications 

__Conclusion__

Summarize the key findings\.

Reinforce the Research Objective\.

__Future Work__

Identify opportunities for future research\.

Examples:

- Additional observations 
- New domains 
- Validation studies 
- Architecture development 

__References__

List all supporting resources\.

Examples:

- Human Frictions 
- Patterns 
- Research Insights 
- Academic references 
- External sources \(if applicable\) 

__Revision History__

Maintain a record of significant updates\.

Example:

__Version__

__Date__

__Description__

1\.0

2026\-07\-26

Initial draft

1\.1

2026\-08\-12

Added new evidence and insights

__6\. Writing Guidelines__

Every Research Paper should:

- Be evidence\-based\. 
- Reference supporting Human Frictions, Patterns, and Research Insights\. 
- Present objective analysis\. 
- Avoid unsupported claims\. 
- Follow the ATLAS Naming Convention\. 
- Maintain clear academic writing\. 

__7\. Final Principle__

__A Research Paper is the highest level of knowledge publication within the ATLAS Research Repository\. It transforms validated observations into structured, evidence\-based contributions that can guide future research, architecture, and innovation\.__
