__ Project UIL__

__ATLAS Standards__

__Research Insight Template__

__Version 1\.0__

__1\. Purpose__

The Research Insight Template defines the standard structure for documenting Research Insights within the ATLAS Research Repository\.

A Research Insight represents an evidence\-based conclusion derived from the analysis of one or more validated Patterns\.

Its purpose is to ensure that every insight is:

- Evidence\-based 
- Traceable 
- Consistent 
- Reusable 
- Suitable for future research and architecture 

Research Insights should explain __what has been learned__, not simply restate observations\.

__2\. Core Principle__

__A Research Insight is a validated conclusion supported by Patterns and Human Frictions\. It explains why recurring Human Frictions exist and what they reveal about human behaviour or system design\.__

Insights should never be based on assumptions or personal opinions\.

__3\. Field Definitions__

__INSID \(Insight Identifier\)__

A permanent and unique identifier assigned to an accepted Research Insight\.

Example:

INS\-001

Once assigned, the identifier never changes\.

__Title__

A concise statement describing the insight\.

Examples:

- Users Prefer Recognition Over Recall 
- Context Switching Significantly Increases Cognitive Load 
- Information Fragmentation Reduces Task Completion Efficiency 

Titles should describe conclusions, not problems\.

__Category__

The primary research domain to which the insight belongs\.

Example:

Digital Experience

__Status__

Current research stage\.

Allowed values:

- Draft 
- Under Review 
- Accepted 
- Archived 
- Retired 

__Confidence Level__

Represents the strength of evidence supporting the insight\.

__Level__

__Meaning__

Level 1

Initial conclusion

Level 2

Moderate evidence

Level 3

Strong evidence

Level 4

Extensive validation

Level 5

Highly established insight

__Version__

Current document version\.

Example:

1\.0

__Created__

Creation date\.

Format:

YYYY\-MM\-DD

__Last Updated__

Most recent revision date\.

__Tags__

Keywords describing the insight\.

Examples:

Cognitive Load

Navigation

Decision Making

Attention

__Supporting Patterns__

List every Pattern supporting the insight\.

Example:

PT\-001

PT\-004

PT\-009

__Supporting APIDs \(Optional\)__

Reference important Human Frictions when necessary\.

Example:

FF\-001

OS\-004

MB\-006

__4\. Research Insight Sections__

__Insight Statement__

Clearly describe the conclusion\.

This section should answer:

__"What have we learned?"__

__Supporting Evidence__

Explain which Patterns support the insight\.

Summarize the evidence rather than repeating individual Human Frictions\.

__Supporting Patterns__

List every supporting Pattern\.

Example:

PT\-001

PT\-004

PT\-009

__Implications__

Explain why this insight matters\.

Examples:

- Better interface design 
- Improved accessibility 
- Reduced cognitive load 
- More effective workflows 

__Research Notes__

Document additional observations\.

Possible contents:

- Related insights 
- Future research questions 
- Limitations 
- Alternative interpretations 

__Revision History__

Maintain a history of significant changes\.

Example:

__Version__

__Date__

__Description__

1\.0

2026\-07\-26

Initial insight documented

1\.1

2026\-08\-15

Added supporting Pattern PT\-007

__6\. Writing Guidelines__

Every Research Insight should:

- Be derived from validated Patterns\. 
- Be supported by evidence\. 
- Explain a meaningful conclusion\. 
- Avoid personal opinions\. 
- Avoid proposing specific solutions\. 
- Reference supporting Patterns\. 
- Follow the ATLAS Naming Convention\. 

__7\. Final Principle__

__A Research Insight transforms recurring Patterns into meaningful knowledge\. It represents what ATLAS has learned through evidence and provides the intellectual foundation for research papers, architectures, and future products\.__
