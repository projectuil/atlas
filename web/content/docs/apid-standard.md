__ Project UIL__

__ATLAS Taxonomy__

__Document 02 - APID Standard__

__Version 1\.0__

__1\. Purpose__

The __Atlas Problem Identifier \(APID\)__ is the permanent, unique identifier assigned to every accepted Human Friction within the ATLAS Research Repository\.

Its purpose is to ensure that every Human Friction can be:

- Uniquely identified 
- Referenced consistently 
- Linked to related research 
- Tracked through its lifecycle 
- Preserved permanently 

An APID represents the __identity of a Human Friction__, not its category or title\.

__2\. Guiding Principle__

__One Human Friction\. One APID\. Forever\.__

Once an APID is assigned:

- It is never reused\. 
- It is never reassigned\. 
- It remains valid even if the title, category, or wording changes\. 

The APID is the permanent identity of the observation\.

__3\. APID Format__

Every APID follows a standardized structure:

<Prefix>\-<Sequential Number>

Examples:

FF\-001

FF\-002

OS\-001

AU\-015

CM\-009

Where:

__Component__

__Description__

Prefix

Category prefix registered in the Prefix Registry

Number

Sequential identifier within that category

Numbers always use __three digits__\.

Future expansion:

FF\-999



FF\-1000

No reset occurs\.

__4\. APID Assignment Lifecycle__

A Human Friction does __not__ receive an APID immediately\.

It progresses through stages\.

Observation



Draft



Review



Accepted



APID Assigned



Published

This ensures only validated observations become permanent records\.

__5\. APID Assignment Criteria__

An APID may be assigned only when all of the following are true:

 The observation describes a genuine Human Friction\.

The problem statement is clear\.

 The observation is solution\-independent\.

 The category has been confirmed\.

 Duplicate search has been completed\.

The entry follows the Human Friction writing standard\.

If any criterion is not met, the observation remains a draft\.

__6\. Duplicate Detection Rule__

Before assigning an APID:

Researchers must determine whether the Human Friction already exists\.

Possible outcomes:

__Exact Duplicate__

No new APID\.

Reference the existing one\.

__Similar Observation__

Expand the existing Human Friction if appropriate\.

__Different Human Friction__

Assign a new APID\.

__7\. APID Permanence__

Once assigned:

The APID must never change\.

Even if:

- The title changes 
- The wording improves 
- The category evolves 
- New evidence is added 

The APID remains constant\.

Example:

Originally

FF\-012

Title:

People forget downloaded file locations\.

Later

Title:

Downloaded files are difficult to locate after downloading\.

The APID remains:

FF\-012

__8\. Retired APIDs__

If a Human Friction is later found to be invalid:

The APID is __retired__, not deleted\.

Example:

FF\-027

Status:

Retired

Reason:

Duplicate of FF\-009

This preserves historical integrity and avoids accidental reuse\.

__9\. APID Migration Rule__

If a Human Friction moves to another category:

__The APID does not change\.__

Example:

Initially:

CM\-018

Later research determines it belongs in Learning\.

It becomes:

Category:  
Learning

APID:

CM\-018

The original identifier is retained, and the move is documented\.

This avoids breaking references in papers, discussions, or linked research\.

__10\. Reserved APIDs__

Numbers may be reserved temporarily for approved work in progress\.

Example:

FF\-041

Status:

Reserved

Expiry:

30 days

If the observation is not completed within the reservation period, the reservation expires\.

Reserved APIDs are not visible in published research\.

__13\. APID Relationships__

One APID may reference others\.

Example:

FF\-009

OS\-012

These references create a network of connected Human Frictions\.

__14\. APID Governance__

Only accepted Human Frictions may receive APIDs\.

Every assignment must be:

- Recorded in the APID Registry 
- Reviewed 
- Logged in the project changelog 

This ensures accountability\.

__15\. Final Principle__

__An APID is more than a number\. It is the permanent research identity of a Human Friction\. It preserves the continuity of knowledge, enables reliable referencing, and forms the backbone of the ATLAS knowledge graph\.__
