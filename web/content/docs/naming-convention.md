__ Project UIL__

__ATLAS Taxonomy__

__Document 03 - Naming Convention__

__Version 1\.0__

__1\. Purpose__

The Naming Convention Standard defines how entities within the ATLAS Research Repository are named\.

Its purpose is to ensure consistency, readability, discoverability, and long\-term maintainability across all research assets\.

This standard applies to:

- Human Frictions 
- Categories 
- Subcategories 
- Patterns 
- Research Insights 
- Research Papers 
- Templates 
- Supporting documentation 

__2\. Core Principle__

__A name should describe reality, not a solution, opinion, or technology\.__

Names should be immediately understandable without additional explanation\.

__3\. Human Friction Naming Rules__

Every Human Friction title must:

- Describe __one__ problem only\. 
- Be written as a factual observation\. 
- Use plain language\. 
- Be solution\-neutral\. 
- Avoid emotional wording\. 
- Avoid product or company names where possible\. 

__Good__

- Downloaded files are difficult to locate after downloading\. 
- Users forget the purpose of old folders\. 
- People lose track of browser tabs during research\. 

__Avoid__

- Bad Downloads 
- Chrome Download Issue 
- Folder Fix 
- Better Search Needed 

__4\. Category Naming Rules__

Category names should:

- Be nouns or noun phrases\. 
- Represent a stable domain\. 
- Use Title Case\. 

Examples:

- Files & Folders 
- Operating System 
- Communication 
- Learning 

__5\. Pattern Naming Rules__

Patterns represent recurring behaviour across multiple Human Frictions\.

Naming format:

Pattern: <Short Descriptive Name>

Examples:

- Pattern: Cognitive Overload 
- Pattern: Context Switching 
- Pattern: Information Fragmentation 

Patterns describe __why__ multiple Human Frictions exist\.

__6\. Research Insight Naming Rules__

Insights are evidence\-based conclusions drawn from one or more Patterns\.

Naming format:

Insight: <Clear Finding>

Examples:

- Insight: Navigation complexity increases with information density\. 
- Insight: Users prioritize recognition over recall in repetitive tasks\. 

__7\. Research Paper Naming Rules__

Research papers should have descriptive, academic\-style titles\.

Examples:

- Understanding Human Frictions in Digital File Management 
- A Taxonomy of Everyday Interaction Frictions 
- From Observation to Architecture: The ATLAS Methodology 

__8\. File Naming Rules__

Use predictable filenames:

FF\-001\.md

PT\-001\.md

INS\-001\.md

RP\-001\.md

Avoid spaces and unnecessary punctuation in filenames\.

__9\. Revision Principle__

Names may evolve for clarity, but changes should preserve meaning\. If a title changes, the APID remains the same and the change is recorded in the revision history\.

__10\. Final Principle__

__Names are the interface to knowledge\. A clear, descriptive, and consistent naming system makes ATLAS easier to understand, search, and expand\.__
