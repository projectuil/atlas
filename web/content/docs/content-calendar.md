__PROJECT UIL CONTENT CALENDAR & GROWTH STRATEGY__

__Version 1\.0__

__1\. Purpose__

This document defines how Project UIL grows through consistent, meaningful publishing\.

The goal is not to maximise followers\.

The goal is to maximise __trust, consistency, and research quality__\.

__2\. Growth Philosophy__

Project UIL grows through:

- Consistent observations 
- High\-quality research 
- Genuine discussions 
- Long\-term thinking 
- Trust 

Not through:

- Virality 
- Clickbait 
- Daily posting pressure 
- Artificial engagement 

__3\. Publishing Schedule__

__Weekly__

Monday

- Atlas Observation 

Wednesday

- Research Pattern / Human Story 

Saturday

- Research Journal / Open Question 

__Three high\-quality posts per week\.__

If a post isn't ready, don't publish it\.

__4\. Monthly Cycle__

__Week 1__

Atlas Observations

__Week 2__

Research Patterns

__Week 3__

Systems Thinking

__Week 4__

Research Journal \+ Milestones

This creates a predictable rhythm without becoming repetitive\.

__5\. Annual Content Themes__

__Quarter 1__

Digital Human Frictions

__Quarter 2__

Physical Human Frictions

__Quarter 3__

System\-Level Human Frictions

__Quarter 4__

Research Reflections & Future Directions

Themes can evolve as the project grows\.

__6\. Growth Strategy__

Growth is measured in four dimensions:

__Knowledge Growth__

- Number of Human Frictions documented 
- Number of Atlas categories 
- Research depth 

__Community Growth__

- Thoughtful comments 
- Meaningful conversations 
- Researchers following the project 

__Brand Growth__

- Recognition of Project UIL 
- Consistent visual identity 
- Invitations to collaborate or discuss 

__Impact Growth__

- Research reused in future work 
- New questions inspired by the content 
- Better understanding of human problems 

__7\. Success Metrics__

Track monthly:

- Posts published 
- Human Frictions added to ATLAS 
- Meaningful comments 
- Conversations started 
- New professional connections 
- Research milestones 

Avoid making follower count the primary measure of success\.

__8\. Community Engagement__

After publishing:

- Reply thoughtfully to comments\. 
- Thank people for sharing experiences\. 
- Ask follow\-up questions\. 
- Record valuable observations that emerge from discussions\. 

Every conversation can become research input\.

__9\. Quarterly Review__

Every three months, review:

- Which posts sparked meaningful discussion? 
- Which topics need deeper research? 
- Are we staying true to the mission? 
- Is the visual identity still consistent? 
- What should improve next quarter? 

Document the answers\.

__10\. Content Backlog__

Maintain three lists:

__Ready__

Posts that have been reviewed and can be published\.

__Draft__

Ideas still being developed\.

__Observation Queue__

Raw observations waiting to become posts\.

This prevents last\-minute writing\.

__11\. Research\-to\-Content Workflow__

Observe

      

Validate

      

Add to ATLAS

      

Choose Content Type

      

Write Draft

      

Design Visual

      

Review

      

Publish

      

Community Discussion

      

Archive

ATLAS always comes __before__ social media\.

__12\. Milestone Strategy__

Celebrate milestones that reflect research, such as:

- 100 Human Frictions 
- 250 Human Frictions 
- New ATLAS category completed 
- Research paper published 
- One year of Project UIL 

Avoid celebrating vanity metrics like follower counts unless they directly support the mission\.

__13\. Long\-Term Growth Roadmap__

__Year 1__

Establish a consistent research voice\.

__Years 2-3__

Develop a recognised body of research and thoughtful discussions\.

__Years 4-5__

Expand into publications, talks, collaborations, or products informed by the research\.

__14\. Final Principle__

__Growth is a consequence of consistent, meaningful research-not the primary objective\.__
