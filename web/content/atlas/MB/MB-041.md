Complex mobile workflows require navigating deep into one app, switching to another, finding relevant info, and returning. Because the OS treats every app as an isolated full-screen portal, there is no spatial or visual bridging. The user bears 100% of the cognitive load of carrying context across these digital borders.

**Human Impact**

*   Mental exhaustion when synthesizing data.
*   High rate of task abandonment when the cognitive load exceeds short-term memory limits.
*   The "what was I doing again?" phenomenon upon returning to the primary app.

**Context**

Commonly occurs when:

*   Planning a trip (switching between Airbnb, Google Maps, a messaging thread, and Safari).
*   Troubleshooting an issue by switching between a customer support chat and device settings.

**Evidence / Observation**

When forced to multitask across three apps, user execution speed drops by over 70% compared to a desktop, heavily marked by hesitation and redundant switching to re-verify data they just read.

**Research Notes**

Focuses on the absence of "workspace" paradigms in mobile OS design, which treats apps as discrete tools rather than components of a holistic workflow.
