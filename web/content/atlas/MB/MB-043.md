Mobile apps update silently in the background. Users frequently open a daily-use app to find that buttons have moved, navigation bars have shifted, or core features are hidden behind new icons. This unprompted disruption immediately breaks entrenched spatial muscle memory.

**Human Impact**

*   Immediate spike in frustration and productivity loss.
*   Feeling of lost ownership or control over personal tools.
*   Reluctance to update other applications out of fear of further disruptive redesigns.

**Context**

Commonly occurs when:

*   Major tech companies push global redesigns to unify aesthetics.
*   Apps pivot business models, replacing utility tabs with monetization or algorithmic feed tabs.

**Evidence / Observation**

Users immediately flood app store reviews with 1-star ratings complaining that "they moved the search bar," demonstrating how heavily mobile efficiency relies on static spatial layout.

**Research Notes**

Validates the friction of forced continuous deployment in consumer software, where iterative A/B testing overrides user stability.
