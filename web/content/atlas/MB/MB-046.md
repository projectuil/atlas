Because mobile apps function as competitive, walled gardens, they resist interoperability. A user cannot seamlessly drag a client's address from a CRM app into a ride-sharing app; they must highlight, copy, switch apps, open a search bar, and paste, acting as the manual bridge between disconnected APIs.

**Human Impact**

*   Tedious, repetitive manual data transfer.
*   Heightened error rate during copy/pasting across multiple apps.
*   Slower operational throughput compared to desktop integration tools.

**Context**

Commonly occurs when:

*   Managing logistics, requiring integration between calendar, mapping, and communication tools.
*   Expense reporting, requiring passing data from a camera, to a banking app, to accounting software.

**Evidence / Observation**

Users routinely maintain a separate physical notebook on their desk just to write down a code or address from App A so they can type it into App B, circumventing the device's entire digital ecosystem.

**Research Notes**

Focuses on the lack of standard inter-process communication (IPC) frameworks exposed to the end-user on mobile platforms.
