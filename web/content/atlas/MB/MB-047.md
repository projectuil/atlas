When battery levels drop, users must manually orchestrate their device's survival. They activate "Low Power Modes" that silently break background fetching, dim screens to unreadable levels, and throttle processors, fundamentally crippling the device's functionality to keep it alive.

**Human Impact**

*   Persistent "battery anxiety" dominating decision making.
*   Missing critical emails because background fetching was disabled to save power.
*   Poor user experience directly enforced by the user to prevent device death.

**Context**

Commonly occurs when:

*   Traveling without access to charging infrastructure.
*   Late in the evening after heavy GPS or camera usage.

**Evidence / Observation**

Users at 15% battery will proactively shut down all network connectivity and turn brightness to zero, rendering a $1,000 piece of technology functionally useless just to preserve the ability to make a single emergency call later.

**Research Notes**

Demonstrates the overarching anxiety generated by tethering all modern communication, finance, and navigation to a single, finite lithium-ion cell.
