Mobile devices display highly sensitive data (bank balances, private chats, medical records) on bright, high-resolution screens in crowded public spaces. Users constantly fight the physical environment (shoulder surfing) to protect digital privacy.

**Human Impact**

*   Avoidance of completing sensitive or urgent tasks while commuting.
*   Paranoia and physical guarding of the device.
*   Accidental exposure of confidential corporate or personal data to bystanders.

**Context**

Commonly occurs when:

*   Viewing banking details or private messages on airplanes, buses, or crowded cafes.
*   Entering passwords or PIN codes in dense lines.

**Evidence / Observation**

Users are routinely observed aggressively dimming their screens to minimum brightness, tilting their phones at unnatural angles, or physically shielding the device with their body to read a text message in public.

**Research Notes**

Explores the physical/digital boundary where hardware form-factor dictates privacy vulnerabilities.
