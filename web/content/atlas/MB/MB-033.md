Mobile viewports cannot display long web forms comprehensively. When users encounter complex requirements (e.g., multi-page tax documents, detailed job applications), the lack of global context, combined with the on-screen keyboard blocking half the screen, makes data entry tedious and error-prone.

**Human Impact**

*   High abandonment rates for mobile checkouts or registrations.
*   Frustration when a validation error occurs off-screen and the user cannot find it.
*   Errors introduced by accidental taps on neighboring drop-down elements.

**Context**

Commonly occurs when:

*   Applying for government services or insurance claims via a mobile browser.
*   Completing medical intake history forms prior to an appointment.

**Evidence / Observation**

Users will start filling out a form on their phone, hit a page with a 15-item scrolling dropdown list and multiple text areas, sigh, copy the URL, and email it to themselves to finish on a desktop.

**Research Notes**

Focuses on the need for conversational interfaces or progressive disclosure models rather than mimicking desktop PDF structures on mobile.
