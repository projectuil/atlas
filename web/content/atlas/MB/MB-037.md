Information flows into a mobile device across dozens of vectors (SMS, WhatsApp, Slack, Teams, Email, Instagram DMs). Because there is no universal, unified search interface that can deeply index all sandboxed third-party apps, users must rely on fallible human memory to guess _which_ app contains a specific conversation or link.

**Human Impact**

*   Wasted time manually opening and searching five different messaging apps for one link.
*   Frustration with the fragmented nature of modern digital communication.
*   Accidental duplication of questions to peers ("Did you send that to me on Slack or email?").

**Context**

Commonly occurs when:

*   Trying to retrieve an article a friend sent a few days ago.
*   Searching for a specific client address that was shared somewhere in a multi-channel project.

**Evidence / Observation**

A user knows exactly what a message said but spends 10 minutes cycling through iMessage, WhatsApp, and Instagram DMs trying to find the context bubble where it was actually delivered.

**Research Notes**

Points to the necessity of OS-level unified knowledge graphs that supersede app-level sandboxes for search indexing.
