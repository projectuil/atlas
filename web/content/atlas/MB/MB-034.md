Mobile devices constantly move through varying zones of connectivity (5G to 3G to Wi-Fi to dead zones). Applications rarely communicate these state changes clearly, resulting in infinite loading spinners, unsaved data, or silent failures that confuse the user regarding the app's operational status.

**Human Impact**

*   Loss of critical data typed during a momentary network drop.
*   Confusion when an app works perfectly in the office but becomes completely unresponsive in the elevator.
*   Frantic tapping of "Submit" buttons that provide no offline queuing feedback.

**Context**

Commonly occurs when:

*   Commuting on public transit (subways, trains) with intermittent signal.
*   Moving from a strong Wi-Fi network out into a street with poor cellular handover.

**Evidence / Observation**

A user types a long reply on a social platform while on a train. As they hit send, the train enters a tunnel. The app shows a spinning wheel forever, and upon forced restart, the entire drafted message is gone.

**Research Notes**

Validates the necessity of "Offline First" architecture and transparent state-syncing UI indicators.
