When a mobile device experiences rapid battery drain, the operating system's native battery diagnostics often provide technical or abstracted data that fails to clearly identify the rogue application, especially if the drain occurs via background API calls.

**Human Impact**

*   Anxiety over device reliability during travel or long shifts.
*   Arbitrary force-closing of all applications in an attempt to stop the drain.
*   Misplaced blame on the hardware battery health rather than unoptimized software.

**Context**

Commonly occurs when:

*   A newly installed app or recent update introduces a background tracking bug.
*   Traveling through areas with poor cellular reception, causing hidden network polling.

**Evidence / Observation**

Users frequently stare at system "Battery Usage" graphs but cannot interpret whether an app using "15% of battery in background" is normal behavior or a critical error, leading them to blindly uninstall useful apps.

**Research Notes**

Highlights the gap between system-level telemetry and user-actionable hardware diagnostics.
