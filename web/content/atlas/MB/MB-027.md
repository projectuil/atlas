Mobile apps persistently cache media, operating systems download updates automatically, and cameras capture high-resolution files. Users are abruptly halted by "Storage Full" warnings but lack granular tools to quickly identify and clear hidden cache bloat without deleting personal memories.

**Human Impact**

*   Panic when unable to take photos during important life events due to full storage.
*   Unintentional deletion of important apps or files to quickly free up space.
*   Chronic dismissal of crucial OS security updates due to insufficient room.

**Context**

Commonly occurs when:

*   Using messaging apps (like WhatsApp or Telegram) that automatically download all received media locally.
*   Owning devices with baseline, non-expandable storage capacities.

**Evidence / Observation**

Users attempt to free space by deleting dozens of photos, only to realize that invisible app cache from social media applications is actually consuming 10x more space than their camera roll.

**Research Notes**

Underscores the need for intelligent, automated cache-clearing mechanisms that do not require manual user auditing.
