Users often struggle to understand what occurs during software installation. Installation wizards present options such as installation paths, permissions, shortcuts, licenses, and additional components, yet many users cannot determine their purpose. As a result, software is frequently installed by repeatedly selecting default options without understanding the consequences of those choices.

**Human Impact**

*   Increased uncertainty during software installation.
*   Incorrect installation decisions.
*   Dependence on default settings.
*   Fear of making mistakes.
*   Reduced confidence managing software.

**Context**

Commonly occurs when:

*   Installing desktop applications.
*   Setting up development software.
*   Installing drivers.
*   Configuring productivity tools.
*   Using installation wizards.

**Evidence / Observation**

This behaviour is consistently observed among beginner and intermediate computer users. Installation processes frequently require decisions that users do not fully understand, such as selecting installation locations, choosing optional components, or granting permissions. Rather than making informed choices, users commonly continue by selecting **Next** until installation is complete.

**Research Notes**

*   Extends OS-006 from conceptual understanding to practical software installation.
*   Highlights limited transparency during installation workflows.
*   Frequently observed across Windows, Linux, and macOS.
*   Likely contributes to future behavioural patterns related to **Installation Decision Making**.
