Users often struggle to understand the role of device drivers in enabling communication between hardware and the operating system. When hardware devices fail to function correctly, users frequently attribute the issue to the device itself rather than recognizing that missing, outdated, or incompatible drivers may be the underlying cause.

**Human Impact**

*   Difficulty using newly connected hardware.
*   Confusion during device setup.
*   Incorrect troubleshooting attempts.
*   Increased dependence on technical assistance.
*   Reduced confidence managing hardware.

**Context**

Commonly occurs when:

*   Connecting printers, webcams, or scanners.
*   Installing graphics cards.
*   Using newly purchased peripherals.
*   Reinstalling the operating system.
*   Updating hardware components.

**Evidence / Observation**

This behaviour is consistently observed across desktop operating systems. Users commonly encounter messages indicating that drivers are missing or incompatible but do not understand their purpose. Questions such as **"Why isn't my device working?"**, **"What is a driver?"**, or **"Do I need to install anything?"** frequently arise because device drivers operate behind the scenes and are rarely explained during normal system use.

**Research Notes**

*   Expands understanding of hardware-software interaction.
*   Complements OS-020 by focusing specifically on device communication.
*   Frequently observed during hardware installation and operating system reinstallation.
*   Likely contributes to future behavioural patterns related to **Device Compatibility Awareness**.
