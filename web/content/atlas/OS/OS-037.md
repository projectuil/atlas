Many users connect multiple monitors to increase productivity and expand their digital workspace. However, effectively distributing applications across displays, remembering where windows are located, and managing workflows between monitors can be challenging. Users frequently move windows between displays, lose track of applications, or experience inconsistent layouts when monitors are disconnected or reconnected.

**Human Impact**

*   Time lost locating windows.
*   Reduced efficiency across multiple displays.
*   Interrupted workflow when monitor layouts change.
*   Increased cognitive effort managing screen locations.
*   Frustration reconnecting external displays.

**Context**

Commonly occurs when:

*   Working with two or more monitors.
*   Connecting laptops to external displays.
*   Programming or designing.
*   Financial analysis.
*   Video editing and content creation.

**Evidence / Observation**

This behaviour is widely observed in professional environments where multiple displays are common. Although operating systems support extended desktops, users often experience difficulty maintaining consistent window placement and workspace organization across displays, particularly after reconnecting monitors or changing hardware configurations.

**Research Notes**

*   Focuses on multi-monitor interaction rather than screen space limitations.
*   Extends workspace management into distributed desktop environments.
*   Future behavioural pattern: **Distributed Workspace Management**.
