When working across multiple devices concurrently (e.g., laptop and tablet side-by-side), authenticating on one device frequently invalidates active sessions on the secondary device due to single-session lock policies.

**Human Impact**

*   Unexpected sign-outs on secondary active screens.
*   Repeated re-authentication cycles as devices kick each other out.
*   Disrupted multi-screen productivity workflows.

**Context**

Commonly occurs in:

*   Streaming services and educational platforms enforcing strict concurrency limits.
*   Financial trading terminals and workstation software.
*   Enterprise security environments with strict concurrent session rules.

**Evidence / Observation**

Users logging into an app on their desktop find that their tablet session—open right next to them for reference—immediately expires and redirects to the sign-in screen.

**Research Notes**

*   Contributes to PAT-002 — Authentication Fatigue.
*   Highlights the clash between traditional single-session token rules and modern multi-device human behavior.
