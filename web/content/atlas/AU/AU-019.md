Complex 2FA/MFA flows demand switching back and forth across multiple apps or devices (e.g., jumping from web browser to email, then to authenticator app, then back to web browser), causing app restarts, lost states, or missed timeouts.

**Human Impact**

*   Severe task interruption and cognitive fragmentation.
*   High rate of dropped authentication attempts on mobile devices.
*   Operational frustration when switching context resets the primary application state.

**Context**

Commonly occurs on:

*   Mobile browsers switching to email/authenticator apps and back.
*   Enterprise SSO requiring hardware tokens or push approvals.
*   Mobile banking identity verifications.

**Evidence / Observation**

On low-memory mobile devices, switching out of the browser to open an authenticator app often forces the browser to refresh, wiping out the active verification screen entirely.

**Research Notes**

*   Contributes to PAT-002 — Authentication Fatigue.
*   Highlights issues with multi-device and cross-app context management.
