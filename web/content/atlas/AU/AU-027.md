Users are unable to easily check if they previously registered for a platform, leading them to create duplicate accounts under alternate emails or social logins, which fragments their personal data, transaction history, and settings.

**Human Impact**

*   Fragmentation of purchases, history, and settings across duplicate profiles.
*   Confusion when logged-in states do not display expected historical data.
*   Difficulty consolidating subscriptions or merging duplicate identities.

**Context**

Commonly occurs during:

*   Rapid guest checkout transitions on e-commerce sites.
*   Joining SaaS tools using work vs. personal credentials.
*   Re-visiting platforms after extended periods of inactivity.

**Evidence / Observation**

Users often discover duplicate accounts only after attempting a purchase or sign-up, being told "Account already exists," and realizing they have two distinct profiles registered under different emails.

**Research Notes**

*   Contributes to PAT-002 — Authentication Fatigue.
*   Points to a failure in proactive identity resolution and account linking mechanisms.
