Users confuse logging in successfully (authentication) with having permission to view specific resources (authorization), causing extreme confusion when an authenticated screen returns "Access Denied" or "403 Forbidden".

**Human Impact**

*   Confusion over why a successfully signed-in account cannot view a link.
*   Repeated, futile attempts to re-authenticate or reset passwords to fix access issues.
*   Dependency on IT helpdesk support to clarify role-based permission boundaries.

**Context**

Commonly occurs in:

*   Shared team workspaces (Google Drive, Notion, Figma).
*   Enterprise Role-Based Access Control (RBAC) portals.
*   Document management systems.

**Evidence / Observation**

When clicking a restricted team link, users repeatedly re-enter their valid credentials, assuming their sign-in failed rather than recognizing that their account simply lacks the required role or view permission.

**Research Notes**

*   Contributes to PAT-002 — Authentication Fatigue.
*   Points to ambiguous error design that fails to separate _identity verification_ from _permission grants_.
