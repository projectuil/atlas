During account setup, users are presented with complex fallback mechanisms (e.g., 16-character backup codes, security questions) without clear context on when or how these items will be needed, causing them to skip or discard them.

**Human Impact**

*   Ignored setup steps leading to unrecoverable accounts during emergencies.
*   Storing sensitive recovery codes insecurely (e.g., raw screenshots, unencrypted notes).
*   Frustration with security questions that are forgotten over time.

**Context**

Commonly occurs during:

*   Initial 2FA/MFA enrollment.
*   High-security service setup (cryptocurrency, banking, cloud infrastructure).
*   Primary email account creation.

**Evidence / Observation**

Users frequently dismiss backup code download prompts or take a hasty screenshot that is subsequently lost, treating the step as an unnecessary nuisance rather than vital insurance.

**Research Notes**

*   Contributes to PAT-002 — Authentication Fatigue.
*   Demonstrates poor onboarding education regarding account fallback mechanics.
