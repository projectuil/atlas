Complex forms ask users to re-type identical information across different sections of the _same page or workflow_ (e.g., billing address vs. shipping address; applicant email vs. emergency contact email) without providing copy-over shortcuts.

**Human Impact**

*   Immediate fatigue and irritation during form filling.
*   Higher rate of data entry typos caused by repetitive typing.
*   Substantial drop in overall completion velocity.

**Context**

Commonly occurs on:

*   E-commerce checkout flows lacking a "Same as billing" checkbox.
*   Insurance claims and legal document intake forms.
*   Visa and travel authorization applications.

**Evidence / Observation**

Observational testing shows user drop-off increases significantly when multi-page forms fail to pre-fill known details entered during preceding steps.

**Research Notes**

*   Contributes to PAT-002 — Authentication Fatigue.
*   Directly violates basic interaction design patterns regarding data propagation.
