Login and verification processes sometimes lack clear confirmation feedback (such as landing back on the requested page without a success message, hanging loading spinners, or blank redirects), leaving users unsure if they are signed in.

**Human Impact**

*   Hesitation and uncertainty about system state.
*   Repeatedly clicking "Sign In" or refreshing, risking double submissions.
*   Confusion when redirected to generic homepages rather than the target content.

**Context**

Commonly occurs in:

*   OAuth / SSO pop-up window redirects.
*   Magic link authentication from mobile email clients.
*   Enterprise portals with multi-layer redirects.

**Evidence / Observation**

Users frequently report re-clicking login links because the redirect back to the app yields no visible change in interface state for several seconds.

**Research Notes**

*   Contributes to PAT-002 — Authentication Fatigue.
*   Directly violates the fundamental UX rule: Visibility of System Status.
