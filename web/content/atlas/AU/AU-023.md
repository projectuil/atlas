Forms reject input strings that appear completely correct to human eyes due to rigid server formatting rules (e.g., auto-rejecting spaces in credit card numbers, hyphens in phone numbers, or trailing spaces in emails).

**Human Impact**

*   Confusion over why accurate data is deemed invalid.
*   Trial-and-error editing to guess the system's expected formatting.
*   Irritation at intolerant visual validation rules.

**Context**

Commonly occurs in inputs for:

*   Credit card numbers.
*   Phone numbers and postal codes.
*   Identification or reference numbers.

**Evidence / Observation**

Users copy-paste clean inputs containing a trailing space or hyphen, receiving an "Invalid Entry" block without dynamic automatic trimming or formatting by the system.

**Research Notes**

*   Contributes to PAT-002 — Authentication Fatigue.
*   Reflects poor system flexibility; inputs should accept human-friendly formats and sanitize them server-side.
