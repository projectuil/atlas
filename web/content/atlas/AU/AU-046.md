Updating a primary email or phone number in one place often breaks authentication flows on secondary connected services, locking users out because verification tokens continue sending to retired contact addresses.

**Human Impact**

*   Permanent loss of account access following life changes (job transition, changing mobile carriers).
*   Complex multi-step manual support verification processes.
*   Stress and anxiety over orphaned personal data and subscriptions.

**Context**

Commonly occurs during:

*   Changing jobs and losing access to corporate email addresses.
*   Changing phone numbers without first updating 2FA settings.
*   Changing legal names or primary personal emails.

**Evidence / Observation**

Users change their primary email on a main account, but discover months later that sub-services or linked platforms still route password reset codes to their old, inactive inbox.

**Research Notes**

*   Contributes to PAT-002 — Authentication Fatigue.
*   Emphasizes the risk of lifecycle changes within decentralized identity management systems.
