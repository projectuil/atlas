Discrepancies in verification depth across platforms (e.g., site A needs only email; site B needs photo ID, SSN/tax ID, and utility bill) confuse users about regulatory compliance versus invasive data harvesting.

**Human Impact**

*   Sudden anxiety when lightweight services suddenly demand heavy government ID checks.
*   Suspicion and reluctance to provide requested documentation.
*   Drop-offs when regulatory verification feels disproportionate to the service value.

**Context**

Commonly occurs during:

*   Onboarding into fintech, crypto, or banking platforms (KYC procedures).
*   Age-restricted product or service access.
*   Gig-economy worker identity checks.

**Evidence / Observation**

Users frequently abandon onboarding when requested to upload live selfies or passport scans, citing lack of clarity on why a digital service requires government-grade identity verification.

**Research Notes**

*   Contributes to PAT-002 — Authentication Fatigue.
*   Points to a need for explicit regulatory context messaging during Know Your Customer (KYC) flows.
