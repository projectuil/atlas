Users feel caught in a constant trade-off between choosing convenient practices (weak/reused passwords, staying logged in on shared devices) and maintaining security (complex unique passwords, strict 2FA), leading to risky compromises.

**Human Impact**

*   Psychological conflict between ease of access and safety.
*   Guilt over utilizing poor security habits to save time.
*   Susceptibility to security incidents due to choosing convenience.

**Context**

Commonly occurs when:

*   Managing dozens of low-priority personal accounts alongside sensitive banking/email profiles.
*   Setting up personal devices used occasionally by family members.

**Evidence / Observation**

Users intentionally disable available security options (like 2FA) whenever platforms allow it, explicitly prioritizing immediate speed over protection.

**Research Notes**

*   Contributes to PAT-002 — Authentication Fatigue.
*   Identifies the core tension in modern identity design: friction vs. security.
