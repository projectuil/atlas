When a form submission yields an error or the user accidentally reloads/navigates away, the page resets and clears all filled text fields, forcing complete re-typing.

**Human Impact**

*   Extreme user frustration and anger.
*   High probability of immediate task abandonment.
*   Time wasted re-entering long text responses or personal details.

**Context**

Commonly occurs in:

*   Job application portals.
*   Complex web questionnaires and support tickets.
*   Checkout forms with strict server-side validation.

**Evidence / Observation**

Observational data shows page-clearing errors are the single highest contributor to immediate task rage-quits on long-form web tools.

**Research Notes**

*   Contributes to PAT-002 — Authentication Fatigue.
*   Highlights the importance of local draft auto-saving and state retention during form errors.
