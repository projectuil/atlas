Forms that are poorly adapted for mobile viewports—triggering wrong software keyboards (e.g., text keyboard instead of numeric keypad for phone numbers), microscopic tap targets, or sticky headers blocking inputs—impair mobile data entry.

**Human Impact**

*   High physical effort and frustration typing on small screens.
*   Frequent mis-taps on small radio buttons and drop-down menus.
*   Postponing form completion until returning to a desktop computer.

**Context**

Commonly occurs in:

*   Desktop-centric legacy portals opened on smartphones.
*   Web views embedded inside mobile social media apps.
*   Mobile checkouts lacking native digital wallet integrations (Apple Pay/Google Pay).

**Evidence / Observation**

Users repeatedly tap tiny dropdowns or zoom in manually on desktop-scaled forms, frequently abandoning the workflow if inputs require lengthy text entries without correct keyboard types.

**Research Notes**

*   Contributes to PAT-002 — Authentication Fatigue.
*   Highlights failure to utilize mobile-native input attributes (type="tel", inputmode="numeric").
