Password requirements are frequently hidden until after form submission, or are written in technical jargon, forcing users to submit invalid passwords through a trial-and-error process.

**Human Impact**

*   Repeated failed submission attempts.
*   Unnecessary cognitive friction during registration.
*   User frustration with arbitrary security criteria.

**Context**

Commonly occurs during:

*   User registration and account setup.
*   Forced periodic password updates.
*   Account security updates.

**Evidence / Observation**

Observed whenever systems only display rules after a form fails validation (e.g., "Password must contain a special character"). Users prefer dynamic, real-time checklist indicators.

**Research Notes**

*   Contributes to PAT-002 — Authentication Fatigue.
*   Direct violation of the usability principle of error prevention through clear proactive communication.
