With choices between email/password, social SSO (Google, Apple, Facebook), and passkeys, users frequently forget which exact method or provider they used during registration, creating duplicate accounts or sign-in errors.

**Human Impact**

*   Confusion and cognitive effort trying multiple login pathways.
*   Creation of unintentional duplicate accounts.
*   Misplaced user preferences or purchase history across duplicate profiles.

**Context**

Commonly occurs on:

*   Content platforms and news sites.
*   E-commerce applications.
*   SaaS products offering multiple Social Sign-On buttons.

**Evidence / Observation**

Users frequently click through several SSO providers sequentially until one logs them in or fails, indicating a complete reliance on trial-and-error due to a lack of memory cues or UI feedback.

**Research Notes**

*   Contributes to PAT-002 — Authentication Fatigue.
*   Illustrates the downside of providing flexible login options without identity hint features.
