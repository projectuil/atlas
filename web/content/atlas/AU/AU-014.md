Services frequently request sensitive data (such as phone numbers, birth dates, or physical addresses) without explaining _why_ it is necessary, leaving users suspicious of unnecessary data harvesting.

**Human Impact**

*   Hesitation and loss of trust in the platform.
*   Anxiety over personal data privacy and spam risks.
*   Entering false or placeholder data to bypass requirements.

**Context**

Commonly occurs during:

*   Mobile app sign-ups asking for location or contact access.
*   Simple digital resource downloads (e.g., whitepapers requiring full phone/address).
*   Social networking account registration.

**Evidence / Observation**

Users often enter fake phone numbers (e.g., 000-000-0000) or discard registration entirely when mandatory personal details seem irrelevant to the product's function.

**Research Notes**

*   Contributes to PAT-002 — Authentication Fatigue.
*   Demonstrates the friction caused by a lack of contextual micro-explanations for data collection.
