Web forms often lack visible security cues or trust indicators (e.g., encryption badges, clear data-handling policies), making users feel uncertain about submitting personal or financial data.

**Human Impact**

*   Anxiety over identity theft, fraud, or data breaches.
*   Hesitation during payment or sign-up flows.
*   Abandonment of forms on unfamiliar or unbranded domain names.

**Context**

Commonly occurs on:

*   Independent e-commerce websites.
*   Third-party embedded forms and frames.
*   Survey links hosted on unfamiliar domains.

**Evidence / Observation**

Users explicitly look for trust symbols, lock icons, or recognizable domain brands before submitting sensitive records, abandoning transactions if visual security assurances are lacking.

**Research Notes**

*   Contributes to PAT-002 — Authentication Fatigue.
*   Highlights how visual design directly shapes security perception.
