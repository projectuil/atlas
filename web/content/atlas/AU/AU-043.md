After investing significant effort into filling out a long form, users hit "Submit" only to be unexpectedly blocked by intrusive post-verification steps (e.g., reCAPTCHA image challenges, sudden email verification gates).

**Human Impact**

*   Cognitive disorientation at an unexpected obstacle at the finish line.
*   Anger when aggressive CAPTCHA challenges fail repeatedly.
*   Risk of session abandonment right before completion.

**Context**

Commonly occurs on:

*   Public contact forms and quote requests.
*   High-volume ticketing or reservation checkouts.
*   Account creation submit buttons.

**Evidence / Observation**

Users who complete a 10-field form express immediate frustration when presented with a 3-page visual CAPTCHA challenge upon clicking submit, perceiving it as an artificial penalty.

**Research Notes**

*   Contributes to PAT-002 — Authentication Fatigue.
*   Demonstrates the friction of placing high-barrier bot mitigation steps at the end of a user journey.
