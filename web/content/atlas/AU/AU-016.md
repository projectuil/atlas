Web applications timeout active sessions without warning while users are actively working or temporarily stepped away, locking the interface and frequently causing unsubmitted data to be lost.

**Human Impact**

*   Frustration due to sudden, unannounced interruptions.
*   Loss of un-saved work, requiring painful manual re-entry.
*   Severe productivity degradation.

**Context**

Commonly occurs in:

*   Banking and tax portals.
*   Internal enterprise systems.
*   Content management systems (CMS) and web editors.

**Evidence / Observation**

Users filling out detailed forms hit "Submit" only to be redirected to a blank login page, realizing after logging back in that all typed data was cleared.

**Research Notes**

*   Contributes to PAT-002 — Authentication Fatigue.
*   Points to a lack of graceful session warnings or background state saving.
