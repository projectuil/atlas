When users switch mobile hardware or change phone numbers, they forget to update their underlying 2FA authenticator apps or SMS fallback settings beforehand, leading to total lockout upon old device decommission.

**Human Impact**

*   Sudden lockout from critical accounts upon hardware retirement.
*   Anxiety and extensive administrative overhead during device upgrades.
*   Dependency on slow manual support interventions for account restoration.

**Context**

Commonly occurs during:

*   Annual phone upgrades or carrier switches.
*   Loss or damage of a primary mobile device.
*   Switching hardware across different OS ecosystems (e.g., iOS to Android).

**Evidence / Observation**

Users setup new phones and wipe old ones, only to discover that local authenticator apps or SMS verification codes are still tied to their deactivated hardware or disconnected SIM card.

**Research Notes**

*   Contributes to PAT-002 — Authentication Fatigue.
*   Underscores the danger of brittle, single-device security dependencies without clear pre-decommissioning prompts.
