Error messages displayed upon failed form submission are often vague, overly technical (e.g., "Invalid format"), or disconnected from the specific field that generated the error.

**Human Impact**

*   Confusion on how to resolve the problem.
*   Wasted time scanning the entire form for error indicators.
*   Decreased user confidence and potential form abandonment.

**Context**

Commonly occurs in:

*   Payment processing screens.
*   Form fields requiring specific data formatting (e.g., phone numbers, postal codes).
*   Complex sign-up questionnaires.

**Evidence / Observation**

Users often scroll up and down trying to figure out which input triggered a generic top-of-page error banner like "Submission failed: please fix inputs."

**Research Notes**

*   Contributes to PAT-002 — Authentication Fatigue.
*   Emphasizes the need for contextual, inline microcopy that explains _how_ to fix an issue.
