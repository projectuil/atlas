As services adopt varied sign-in strategies—ranging from passwords and SMS codes to TOTP apps, SSO, magic links, and passkeys—users are overwhelmed trying to remember _how_ each service authenticates them.

**Human Impact**

*   Significant mental overhead keeping track of digital credentials.
*   Inability to seamlessly log in from a new device without trial-and-error.
*   Fear of locking oneself out when changing primary mobile phones or emails.

**Context**

Commonly occurs during:

*   Device upgrades or replacements.
*   Periodic logins to secondary or seldom-used digital accounts.
*   Transferring settings to new platforms.

**Evidence / Observation**

Users routinely forget which accounts rely on a dedicated authenticator app versus SMS or Google Sign-In, leading to failed login attempts when accessing services on new hardware.

**Research Notes**

*   Contributes to PAT-002 — Authentication Fatigue.
*   Demonstrates identity ecosystem fragmentation across modern digital platforms.
