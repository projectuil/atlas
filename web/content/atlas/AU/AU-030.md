When navigating ecosystems with Single Sign-On (SSO) or federated identity, users frequently fail to realize they are already signed in, leading them to unnecessarily seek out sign-in forms or attempt manual re-login.

**Human Impact**

*   Redundant efforts trying to log into active sessions.
*   Hesitation when encountering auto-redirect screens.
*   Confusion regarding account status across subdomains or integrated platforms.

**Context**

Commonly occurs within:

*   Corporate intranet suites with multiple internal web tools.
*   Connected products under a single parent ecosystem.
*   Educational portals linking to third-party learning modules.

**Evidence / Observation**

Users manually click "Log In" buttons on connected partner sites, unaware that hovering or navigating directly into the app would automatically leverage their existing active SSO token.

**Research Notes**

*   Contributes to PAT-002 — Authentication Fatigue.
*   Points to inadequate visual indicators of active system-wide authentication states.
