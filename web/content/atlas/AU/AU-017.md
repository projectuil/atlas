The lack of standard rules across the web (e.g., site A bans special characters, site B demands them; site C sets a max length of 16 characters) prevents users from applying consistent, memorized strategies.

**Human Impact**

*   Confusion and cognitive overload managing varied mental rulesets.
*   Higher rate of forgotten passwords on sites with non-standard constraints.
*   Frustration during setup due to trial-and-error password modifications.

**Context**

Commonly occurs when:

*   Registering across multiple independent platforms.
*   Creating accounts on legacy portals with outdated security constraints.

**Evidence / Observation**

Users attempt to enter their default complex password, only to receive errors like "Password cannot contain special characters like '@' or '!'"—directly contradicting advice given by other services.

**Research Notes**

*   Contributes to PAT-002 — Authentication Fatigue.
*   Underlines web-wide inconsistency in security best practice implementation.
