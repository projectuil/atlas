Malicious phishing pages clone legitimate login interfaces down to the exact styling, making it nearly impossible for average users to visually detect credential theft risks without inspecting technical details like the full URL.

**Human Impact**

*   Compromise of personal accounts and financial security.
*   Ongoing anxiety and mistrust regarding legitimate login prompts.
*   Loss of control over digital identity.

**Context**

Commonly occurs via:

*   Links in fraudulent emails or SMS messages.
*   Malicious search engine ad redirects.
*   Spoofed social engineering prompts.

**Evidence / Observation**

Observational testing reveals users prioritize visual layout match over URL bar verification, routinely typing real credentials into pixel-perfect fake login pages.

**Research Notes**

*   Contributes to PAT-002 — Authentication Fatigue.
*   Emphasizes the critical need for phishing-resistant authentication options (such as WebAuthn / Passkeys).
