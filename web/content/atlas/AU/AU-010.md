Forms fail to visually distinguish mandatory fields from optional fields clearly (e.g., subtle asterisks, lack of explicit labels), leading to missing input errors upon form submission.

**Human Impact**

*   Interrupted filling flow.
*   Annoyance at needing to re-read and identify missed inputs.
*   Unnecessary form submission errors.

**Context**

Commonly occurs in:

*   Extensive multi-field checkout pages.
*   Government or administrative applications.
*   Profile customization settings.

**Evidence / Observation**

Users skip unmarked optional fields or omit unspotted required fields, only to be stopped by post-submission validation errors highlighting missing required fields.

**Research Notes**

*   Contributes to PAT-002 — Authentication Fatigue.
*   Points to inadequate visual hierarchy and visual affordance standards in form design.
