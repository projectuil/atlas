Users who are already securely logged into an active session are surprised and frustrated when asked to re-enter their password or biometrics to perform specific sensitive tasks (e.g., changing payout details, viewing keys).

**Human Impact**

*   Irritation at feeling "distrusted" by an active session interface.
*   Task interruption and loss of focus.
*   Abandonment of high-friction security configuration changes.

**Context**

Commonly occurs during:

*   Modifying banking payout or direct deposit preferences.
*   Editing account security details or changing passwords.
*   Exporting sensitive personal data or project files.

**Evidence / Observation**

Users often perceive repeated step-up prompts inside an active session as a system glitch or bug rather than an intentional defense mechanism against session hijacking.

**Research Notes**

*   Contributes to PAT-002 — Authentication Fatigue.
*   Points to a lack of explicit explanatory messaging when invoking step-up authentication.
