Users experience inconsistent authentication outcomes across devices (e.g., working smoothly on mobile via biometrics, but failing on desktop due to missing tokens or strict MFA triggers), leading to confusion about account status.

**Human Impact**

*   Perplexion and loss of faith in system consistency.
*   Inability to resume tasks seamlessly when switching hardware environments.
*   Time wasted troubleshooting passwords when the actual cause is device-specific security rules.

**Context**

Commonly occurs when:

*   Moving from native mobile apps to desktop web browsers.
*   Working across personal laptops and enterprise managed workstations.
*   Utilizing passkeys or local biometric authenticators on unsupported secondary browsers.

**Evidence / Observation**

Users assume password failure when an action is rejected on desktop, unaware that their account requires a risk-based device approval step or biometric token not available on the secondary device.

**Research Notes**

*   Contributes to PAT-002 — Authentication Fatigue.
*   Highlights the cognitive gap between unified platform identity and device-specific security tokens.
