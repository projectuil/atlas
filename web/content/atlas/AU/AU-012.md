Submitting a long form with several incorrect entries returns a wall of error alerts simultaneously, overwhelming the user and making systematically fixing errors exhausting.

**Human Impact**

*   Cognitive overload and frustration.
*   Feeling defeated by the system interface.
*   High rate of session abandonment on lengthy forms.

**Context**

Commonly occurs in:

*   Tax filings or complex registration forms.
*   Job applications.
*   Multi-step survey and onboarding flows.

**Evidence / Observation**

When faced with 5+ red validation alerts at once after hitting submit, user mouse movements stall significantly—reflecting high cognitive load—before either making fixes slowly or closing the tab.

**Research Notes**

*   Contributes to PAT-002 — Authentication Fatigue.
*   Underlines the benefits of live inline validation over batch post-submission validation.
