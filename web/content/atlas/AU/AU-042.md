Minor variations in how users enter personal details (e.g., "Apt 4B" vs "Apartment 4-B", abbreviated middle names) cause backend data mismatch failures when systems cross-reference identity records.

**Human Impact**

*   Unexplained verification rejections during automated identity checks.
*   Effort required to re-submit forms using alternative formatting.
*   Inability to link related accounts across platforms.

**Context**

Commonly occurs during:

*   Credit check and credit card applications.
*   Official government registration or tax filings.
*   Identity verification steps on financial exchanges.

**Evidence / Observation**

Users fail automated background verification because their address typed on a new form slightly differs from the exact string format on their credit bureau record.

**Research Notes**

*   Contributes to PAT-002 — Authentication Fatigue.
*   Points to a lack of address normalization services (e.g., USPS address standardization) on form inputs.
