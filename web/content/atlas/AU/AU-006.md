Users explicitly mark a personal computer or smartphone as a "trusted device," yet continue to encounter full authentication or 2FA checks, leading to confusion over the purpose of the setting.

**Human Impact**

*   Undermined trust in product UI choices ("Remember this device" feels useless).
*   Frustration over unexplained security enforcement.
*   Slower task execution.

**Context**

Commonly occurs in:

*   Banking apps accessed on personal laptops.
*   Enterprise work suites accessed from home networks.
*   Services clearing cookies dynamically upon browser restarts.

**Evidence / Observation**

Users report feeling that checking "Remember me for 30 days" is non-functional, as cookie clearing policies, IP shifts, or aggressive platform timeouts invalidate the token silently.

**Research Notes**

*   Contributes to PAT-002 — Authentication Fatigue.
*   Reflects a disconnect between backend risk engine policies and frontend affordances.
