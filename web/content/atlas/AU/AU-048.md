Users grant third-party apps access via OAuth ("Sign in with...") over time, but lose track of which external apps retain access to their core identity profile, creating unmanaged security risks.

**Human Impact**

*   Unawareness of lingering security access given to old or abandoned third-party apps.
*   Difficulty auditing or revoking third-party identity permissions.
*   Worry over privacy leaks across interconnected service graphs.

**Context**

Commonly occurs in:

*   Social SSO ecosystems (Google, Facebook, Apple, Microsoft accounts).
*   Mobile app integrations authorizing secondary utility tools.
*   Enterprise SaaS app stores and integrations.

**Evidence / Observation**

When shown their account's "Connected Apps" security settings, users are routinely surprised to discover dozens of forgotten services retaining active read/write permissions to their profiles.

**Research Notes**

*   Contributes to PAT-002 — Authentication Fatigue.
*   Highlights the visibility gap in long-term federated token management.
