Digital services operate in silos, requiring users to manually fill out identical core datasets (e.g., work history, basic identity details) into disparate systems that lack data-portability integrations.

**Human Impact**

*   Monotony and operational burnout.
*   Perception that digital tools are inefficient and disconnected.
*   Decreased productivity during repetitive administrative workflows.

**Context**

Commonly occurs during:

*   Job application portals (e.g., re-typing resume details).
*   Healthcare patient intake forms across different providers.
*   Applying for loans or official documents across financial platforms.

**Evidence / Observation**

Users frequently resort to keeping text scratchpads open to manually copy-paste identical information across separate web tabs.

**Research Notes**

*   Contributes to PAT-002 — Authentication Fatigue.
*   Highlights the absence of widespread cross-platform digital identity and data sharing standards.
