Because users frequently own multiple personal, work, or secondary email addresses and phone numbers, they struggle to remember which specific contact identifier was registered with a particular service during sign-up.

**Human Impact**

*   Time lost attempting logins using incorrect email or phone combinations.
*   Frustration receiving "Account not found" or trigger-happy password resets on wrong emails.
*   Increased likelihood of creating accidental duplicate accounts.

**Context**

Commonly occurs on:

*   E-commerce stores visited infrequently.
*   Subscription services and utility platforms.
*   Academic and professional alumni portals.

**Evidence / Observation**

Users frequently cycle through 3-4 email addresses sequentially during login, using trial-and-error until one generates a recognized credential or password reset message.

**Research Notes**

*   Contributes to PAT-002 — Authentication Fatigue.
*   Highlights the lack of clear account discovery mechanisms prior to full authentication.
