When a user is interrupted while completing a multi-page form (e.g., browser crash, lost connectivity, unexpected timeout), systems rarely save progress as drafts, forcing a restart from step one.

**Human Impact**

*   Severe frustration and resentment toward the service.
*   Permanent task abandonment for non-mandatory forms.
*   Wasted cognitive and physical effort re-filling completed fields.

**Context**

Commonly occurs in:

*   Government grant or license applications.
*   Long consumer feedback or job application forms.
*   Complex insurance or banking applications.

**Evidence / Observation**

Users who experience a network disconnect on step 4 of a 5-step form frequently abandon the entire application process rather than start over from step 1.

**Research Notes**

*   Contributes to PAT-002 — Authentication Fatigue.
*   Highlights the absence of client-side local storage draft saving and auto-save mechanisms.
