Platforms that lack multi-account management force users to explicitly log out of one account and re-authenticate into another, making work-life or multi-tenant switching cumbersome and disruptive.

**Human Impact**

*   Workflow friction when toggling between work and personal profiles.
*   Increased likelihood of posting content or completing actions under the wrong account profile.
*   Time wasted completing repetitive full authentication steps.

**Context**

Commonly occurs on:

*   Developer tools and cloud console management platforms.
*   Social media and community platforms.
*   Productivity suites (e.g., cloud storage, email clients).

**Evidence / Observation**

Users regularly employ workaround tactics—such as opening incognito windows or secondary browser profiles—specifically to keep two accounts logged in simultaneously.

**Research Notes**

*   Contributes to PAT-002 — Authentication Fatigue.
*   Emphasizes the need for native multi-session profile switching in complex web applications.
