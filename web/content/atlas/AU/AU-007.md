When account credentials are forgotten, recovery flows are often long, ambiguous, or depend on secondary communication paths (outdated recovery emails or phone numbers) that the user no longer controls.

**Human Impact**

*   High emotional stress and fear of permanent data/asset loss.
*   Extreme delay in recovering access to services.
*   Service abandonment when recovery requires complex verification.

**Context**

Commonly occurs during:

*   Intermittent or seasonal service access.
*   Device changes or phone number updates.
*   Legacy account sign-ins.

**Evidence / Observation**

A primary cause of support ticket volume across consumer services. Users frequently discover they can no longer access the legacy email address configured as their fallback method years prior.

**Research Notes**

*   Contributes to PAT-002 — Authentication Fatigue.
*   Shows how security barriers during recovery can lock out legitimate owners permanently.
