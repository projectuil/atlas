When evaluating products, services, research papers, or documentation across different web platforms, users encounter wildly varying data formats, measurement units, feature descriptions, and pricing structures. Because there is no standard data layout across independent websites, users are forced to manually collect, normalize, and compare information by constantly switching browser tabs or maintaining external notes. This manual synthesis imposes a heavy memory and cognitive burden.

**Human Impact**

*   High cognitive load caused by rapid, repetitive browser tab switching.
*   Increased likelihood of miscalculating costs, feature availability, or specifications.
*   Decision fatigue and prolonged research cycles before taking action.
*   Heavy reliance on manual workaround tools (e.g., spreadsheets, copied notes).
*   Incomplete or inaccurate comparison leading to suboptimal purchases or choices.

**Context**

Commonly occurs when:

*   Comparing flight, hotel, or car rental options across booking platforms.
*   Evaluating software subscription tiers and feature matrices across competitors.
*   Shopping for complex consumer electronics across different retail sites.
*   Researching academic sources or technical solutions across disparate publications.
*   Gathering price quotes for services or utility providers.

**Evidence / Observation**

Observational tracking demonstrates that users routinely open 5 to 15 browser tabs simultaneously when preparing to make a decision involving multiple web vendors. Users spend significant time scrolling up and down individual pages to locate corresponding data points (such as shipping fees, warranty terms, or core specifications) that are structured differently on each platform. Many users actively abandon multi-tab web comparison entirely, relying on third-party aggregator sites or making uninformed decisions to avoid synthesis effort.

**Research Notes**

*   Highlights the lack of interoperability and standardized data representation across the web.
*   Bridges individual page comprehension with multi-platform workflow execution.
*   Directly drives complex tab management behaviors (WI-021).
*   Essential to behavioral patterns related to Multi-Source Comparison, Information Synthesis, and Cross-Platform Evaluation.
