Minimizing a window places it out of sight on the taskbar or dock. When users need to return to that minimized task, they must stop their current line of thought, look down at the taskbar, scan icons or hover previews, click to un-minimize, and wait for the window to re-render, breaking workflow state.

**Human Impact**

*   Frequent micro-interruptions in concentration.
*   Extra physical interactions required to restore minimized apps.
*   Slower workflow execution.

**Context**

Commonly occurs when:

*   Keeping background reference applications or chat windows minimized.
*   Temporarily clearing screen space to focus on a primary document.

**Evidence / Observation**

Users frequently minimize a window to get it out of the way, only to need it 30 seconds later, resulting in repetitive minimize/restore cycles.

**Research Notes**

*   Examines the cost of minimizing state behaviors vs. ambient or spatial visibility.
