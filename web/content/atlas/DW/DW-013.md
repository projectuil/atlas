Over long, extended work sessions, initial window groupings degrade. Opening new links, opening secondary documents, or receiving notifications gradually scatters task-related windows across different screens and stacks, breaking down the user's initial spatial organization.

**Human Impact**

*   Progressive deterioration of workspace order throughout the day.
*   Increased time required to locate task assets as the session continues.
*   Mental fatigue from constantly reorganizing drifted windows.

**Context**

Commonly occurs when:

*   Extended work sessions spanning several hours or days without a full reset.
*   High-volume research involving opening numerous secondary references.

**Evidence / Observation**

A workspace that begins neatly organized at 9:00 AM routinely degenerates into a scattered mix of unrelated windows by 2:00 PM due to lack of system-enforced task grouping.

**Research Notes**

*   Connects to "Digital Workspace Degradation" behavioral patterns.
