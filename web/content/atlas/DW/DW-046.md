Tasks in modern work environments are rarely isolated; sub-task A often feeds data into task B, which depends on a response in task C. Standard operating systems present windows as flat, independent entities, completely concealing functional dependencies, data pipelines, or relationships between active windows.

**Human Impact**

*   Difficulty tracking multi-step workflows across applications.
*   Risk of completing tasks out of order or acting on outdated dependent data.
*   Elevated mental demand to map relationships manually in memory.

**Context**

Commonly occurs when:

*   Executing multi-stage workflows involving data extraction, analysis, transformation, and reporting.
*   Managing dependent tasks across different team communication threads and project boards.

**Evidence / Observation**

Users frequently drop context or forget to update a downstream document after modifying an upstream source because the OS presents no visual representation of dependency links between windows.

**Research Notes**

*   Focuses on visual relational graphing and inter-application state dependency models.
