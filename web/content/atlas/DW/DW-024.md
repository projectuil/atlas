Long work sessions naturally generate substantial digital residue: downloaded files on the desktop background, stale browser windows, abandoned utility applications, and unclosed documents. Operating systems do not offer passive decluttering or lifecycle management tools, leaving the workspace clogged with artifacts from finished tasks.

**Human Impact**

*   Visual chaos and loss of workspace clarity.
*   Difficulty identifying current active files amidst accumulated clutter.
*   Psychological burden and reluctance to begin new tasks on a dirty workspace.

**Context**

Commonly occurs when:

*   Working continuously over multiple days without restarting or performing manual cleanups.
*   Downloading temporary attachments and files directly to the desktop surface.

**Evidence / Observation**

Desktops become littered with dozens of unorganized temporary files and idle background windows that users avoid clearing due to the time required to sort them.
