Because information is scattered across distinct applications (e.g., notes in Notion, chats in Slack, specs in PDF, tasks in Jira), users struggle to recall which specific app holds a needed detail. This forces users into an exhaustive search cycle, opening and querying multiple software applications sequentially.

**Human Impact**

*   High cognitive fatigue caused by searching across multiple application databases.
*   Significant time delays during decision-making or content creation.
*   Reduced confidence in digital information storage.

**Context**

Commonly occurs when:

*   Looking for specific project updates, links, or instructions received earlier.
*   Working across cross-functional team tools.

**Evidence / Observation**

Users routinely open 3 to 4 distinct tools in succession, trying different search queries in each until they locate the needed piece of information.

**Research Notes**

*   Demonstrates the friction caused by siloed application data models.
