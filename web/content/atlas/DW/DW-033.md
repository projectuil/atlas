Workflows requiring rapid window toggling impose a significant cognitive tax known as "switching cost." Each transition forces the brain to disengage from one visual/mental model and orient to another. Over time, continuous switching severely degrades cognitive throughput and focus quality.

**Human Impact**

*   Exponential accumulation of mental fatigue throughout the workday.
*   Increased error rate in data transfer, editing, and decision-making.
*   Inability to achieve a state of deep, uninterrupted focus (flow state).

**Context**

Commonly occurs when:

*   Cross-referencing data across split-screen or stacked environments without unified views.
*   Managing real-time communications while attempting long-form composition or coding.

**Evidence / Observation**

Studies measure a re-orientation latency of several seconds every time a user switches windows, compounding into massive lost time over hundreds of switches per day.

**Research Notes**

*   Directly addresses cognitive re-orientation overhead in window-based GUI paradigms.
