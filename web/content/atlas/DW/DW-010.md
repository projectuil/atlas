While adding physical monitors increases screen area, OS environments treat each display as isolated containers with distinct taskbars, scaling parameters, and snapping bounds. Users struggle to maintain unified task context across monitor boundaries, treating secondary displays as "dumping grounds" rather than integrated extensions of a single workspace.

**Human Impact**

*   Broken visual and cognitive continuity across physical displays.
*   Difficulty tracking mouse cursor position and active window focus.
*   Inefficient spatial allocation across primary and secondary screens.

**Context**

Commonly occurs when:

*   Operating workstations equipped with 2 or more external displays.
*   Docking and undocking laptops from desktop monitor setups.

**Evidence / Observation**

Users frequently report losing track of cursor locations, having dialog boxes spawn on secondary screens, or leaving forgotten windows idling on auxiliary displays.

*   Key entry for hardware-software interaction research in multi-screen environments.
