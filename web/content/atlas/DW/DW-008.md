System updates, crashes, or daily shutdowns clear the visual layout of open windows. Upon rebooting, operating systems rarely restore application positions, active tabs, virtual desktop distributions, and document scroll locations with complete fidelity, forcing users to manually reconstruct their entire workspace.

**Human Impact**

*   Delay and mental friction at the start of a workday or after a reboot.
*   Loss of working context and transient ideas.
*   Frustration with unpredictable session restoration.

**Context**

Commonly occurs when:

*   System restarts due to OS updates.
*   Turning on a desktop/laptop at the start of the working day.

**Evidence / Observation**

Users spend 5 to 15 minutes every morning reopening apps, dragging them to specific monitors, and arranging windows back into their preferred working configuration.

**Research Notes**

*   Focuses on workspace state persistence across system power cycles.
