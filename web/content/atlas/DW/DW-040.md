Users spend considerable effort customizing window placement, dock setups, pin states, and app arrangements on their primary computer. When migrating to a new machine or working on a secondary system (e.g., desktop vs. laptop), desktop layout preferences do not sync automatically, forcing users to manually rebuild their setup.

**Human Impact**

*   Reduced productivity when working away from primary workstations.
*   Frustration with re-configuring preferences on secondary machines.
*   Inconsistent spatial muscle memory across hardware endpoints.

**Context**

Commonly occurs when:

*   Switching between office desktop computers and remote work laptops.
*   Provisioning new workstations or replacing old hardware.

**Evidence / Observation**

Users often work far less efficiently on secondary machines because their habitual window positions and tool alignments are absent and tedious to recreate manually.

**Research Notes**

*   Focuses on cloud-synchronized workspace configurations and environment portability.
