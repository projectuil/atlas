Communication tools (Slack, Teams, Email, WhatsApp) are integral to modern work, yet they operate on an asynchronous delivery model that constantly pushes unsolicited visual and audible interruptions onto the desktop. Existing "Do Not Disturb" toggles are often too binary or require manual activation, leaving users vulnerable to continuous focus degradation.

**Human Impact**

*   Severe fragmentation of deep work blocks.
*   Anxiety surrounding unread message counters and pop-up banners.
*   Reluctance to close communication apps due to fear of missing urgent updates (FOMO).

**Context**

Commonly occurs when:

*   Attempting deep focus work (coding, writing, analysis) in collaborative team settings.
*   Presenting screens or working against tight deadlines.

**Evidence / Observation**

Users receive an average of dozens to hundreds of desktop notifications per day, causing them to abandon deep focus tasks every 6–10 minutes on average.
