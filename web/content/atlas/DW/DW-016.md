External interruptions (e.g., an urgent call, incoming message, or ad-hoc request) force users to reconfigure their desktop windows to address the immediate priority. Once the interruption ends, users struggle to manually re-establish the exact set of windows, documents, and reference materials they had open previously.

**Human Impact**

*   Long recovery latency after interruptions.
*   Context loss and forgotten sub-tasks.
*   Re-work required to locate previously open documents.

**Context**

Commonly occurs when:

*   Handling urgent customer support or manager requests during deep focus work.
*   Joining unscheduled online meetings that require screen sharing.

**Evidence / Observation**

After an interruption, users spend several minutes re-navigating to folder locations, reopening closed documents, and moving windows back to where they were prior to the disruption.
