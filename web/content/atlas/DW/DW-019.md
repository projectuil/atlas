Many users utilize single physical computers for both work and personal activities. Operating systems lack strict boundaries between these domains on a single logged-in desktop, leading to personal messaging apps, personal browser tabs, and work documents intermingling on the same taskbars and desktop layouts.

**Human Impact**

*   Privacy risks during screen sharing or presentation sessions.
*   Blur of work-life boundaries leading to continuous distraction.
*   Accidental sharing or pasting of personal links into work channels.

**Context**

Commonly occurs when:

*   Remote or hybrid workers using personal hardware or working from home.
*   Conducting screen-share presentations while personal messaging applications remain running.

**Evidence / Observation**

Users routinely scramble to close personal tabs, messaging clients, or private files right before sharing their screen on a professional conference call.

**Research Notes**

*   Relates to boundary management, privacy, and identity management in desktop design.
