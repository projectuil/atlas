Unexpected system reboots caused by kernel panics, battery drain, power outages, or forced OS updates drop the entire active state of a desktop workspace. Standard crash recovery solutions rarely restore multi-window layouts, active scroll positions, unsaved text buffers, or split-screen positions accurately.

**Human Impact**

*   Severe anxiety and disruption caused by abrupt context destruction.
*   Hours lost manually re-opening, re-arranging, and re-identifying active work files.
*   Risk of complete data loss for transient or unsaved information.

**Context**

Commonly occurs when:

*   Experiencing OS-level instability, forced update reboots, or hardware power loss.
*   Working with complex setups involving many open web apps and local files.

**Evidence / Observation**

Following an unplanned reboot, users frequently spend up to 30 minutes tracing browser histories and recently opened files to recreate their lost mental and visual environment.

**Research Notes**

*   Emphasizes the vulnerability of volatile UI states across unexpected failure modes.
