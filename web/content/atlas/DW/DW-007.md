Standard desktop environments do not dynamically adapt window sizes to the content or context of a task. Consequently, users are forced to continuously grab window borders to manually stretch, shrink, align, or drag application windows throughout their active work hours.

**Human Impact**

*   Accumulated micro-frictions and physical mouse effort.
*   Ongoing distraction from the core mental task.
*   Inefficient utilization of screen real estate.

**Context**

Commonly occurs when:

*   Trying to view two documents side-by-side.
*   Adjusting chat or reference windows next to a primary workspace.

**Evidence / Observation**

Users expend dozens of minor drag-and-drop adjustments per hour simply attempting to align window boundaries cleanly on screen.

**Research Notes**

*   Uncovers micro-friction behavior in window management ergonomics.
