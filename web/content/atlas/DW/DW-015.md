When multiple active application windows, sidebars, blinking cursors, video feeds, and notification badges are visible on screen simultaneously, the user's visual field becomes saturated. This visual noise causes cognitive fatigue and makes it difficult to maintain deep concentration on the primary work document.

**Human Impact**

*   Eye strain and heightened mental fatigue.
*   Increased susceptibility to peripheral digital distractions.
*   Reduced ability to enter or maintain deep focus state.

**Context**

Commonly occurs when:

*   Working on high-resolution or ultra-wide monitors displaying multiple open applications.
*   Receiving real-time messaging updates while trying to draft complex material.

**Evidence / Observation**

Eye-tracking and observational studies demonstrate user gaze continually drifting toward background windows, active scrollbars, or unread indicator badges visible in peripheral vision.

**Research Notes**

*   Tightly coupled with visual ergonomics and cognitive load theory.
