To keep their screen visual area clean, users frequently close applications as soon as a sub-task finishes. However, because workflows are non-linear, users are forced to repeatedly reopen the exact same applications, navigate back to specific files, and re-establish configurations multiple times in a single day.

**Human Impact**

*   Accumulated physical effort from redundant app navigation and launching.
*   Loss of speed due to repeated cold-start loading times.
*   Heightened frustration with workspace management overhead.

**Context**

Commonly occurs when:

*   Intermittently accessing reference tools, calculators, or communication suites.
*   Attempting to maintain a minimal desktop footprint on small screens.

**Evidence / Observation**

Observational data reveals users launching and quitting primary applications like PDF viewers or terminal windows upwards of 10–20 times per shift.

**Research Notes**

*   Demonstrates the trade-off users force upon themselves between visual clarity and launch readiness.
