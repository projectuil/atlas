Variations in physical keyboard design—such as key pitch, actuation distance, tactile force, keycap shape, and travel depth—disrupt motor adaptation across different devices (e.g., ultra-thin laptops vs. deep mechanical keyboards vs. tablet folios).

**Human Impact**

*   Typing awkwardness and frequent miss-hits when moving between devices.
*   Inconsistent tactile feedback leading to missed key actuations or double presses.
*   Discomfort and hand fatigue caused by adapting to varying key resistance.

**Context**

Commonly occurs when:

*   Moving between a desktop mechanical keyboard and a thin laptop keyboard.
*   Using tablet smart keyboards or compact 60% keyboards.

**Evidence / Observation**

Keystroke timing variations indicate a calibration delay of 5–15 minutes when users switch physical keyboard types before reaching baseline speed.

**Research Notes**

*   Establishes hardware haptic variance limits.
*   Informs dynamic debouncing and key sensitivity tuning.
