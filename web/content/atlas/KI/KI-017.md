Physical keyboard layouts vary significantly across regions (ANSI vs ISO, QWERTY vs AZERTY/QWERTZ) and hardware manufacturers (MacBook vs PC desktop vs Compact 60%). When users operate a different computer or travel abroad, established muscle memory fails, resulting in constant typing errors and physical disorientation.

**Human Impact**

*   Severe drop in typing speed and visual confidence upon switching hardware.
*   Frequent mispresses of symbol keys, Enter, Shift, and Control.
*   Mental exhaustion caused by constantly verifying key labels.

**Context**

Commonly occurs when:

*   Traveling internationally and using local public or workplace terminals.
*   Switching between personal MacBook and corporate Windows laptop.
*   Sharing workstation desktop setups in hybrid office environments.

**Evidence / Observation**

Observations in shared workstation environments indicate that users experience significant error bursts during their first 15–30 minutes of interaction with an unfamiliar physical keyboard layout or keycap mechanism.

**Research Notes**

*   Documents spatial-motor disruption caused by non-standardized hardware manufacturing.
*   Informs dynamic software key-remapping tools.
