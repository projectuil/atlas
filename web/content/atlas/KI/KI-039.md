Lock keys toggle system-wide modal states without strong visual indicators. Users frequently activate them by accident, leading to corrupted text entry (CAPS LOCK), disabled arrow keys on numpads (Num Lock), or unexpected spreadsheet scrolling (Scroll Lock).

**Human Impact**

*   Confusion when keys produce unexpected outputs without obvious cause.
*   Password entry failures due to silent Caps Lock activation.
*   Disruption required to diagnose and disable unwanted modal lock states.

**Context**

Commonly occurs when:

*   Entering passwords in obscured text fields.
*   Navigating Excel spreadsheets when Scroll Lock is inadvertently enabled.

**Evidence / Observation**

Password authentication logs show that Caps Lock state misalignment accounts for a substantial percentage of initial login failures across web services.

**Research Notes**

*   Evaluates modal hardware state hazards and non-intrusive status indicators.
