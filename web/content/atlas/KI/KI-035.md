Power shortcuts like Ctrl+Backspace (delete word), Ctrl+Shift+K (delete line), or Ctrl+A + keypress (overwrite selection) can instantly destroy large blocks of text. When executed accidentally, users panic and struggle to safely restore deleted content.

**Human Impact**

*   Anxiety and panic over sudden data loss.
*   Accidental overwriting of undo buffers by pressing additional keys in confusion.
*   Lost time spent manually retyping destroyed content.

**Context**

Commonly occurs when:

*   Reaching for Backspace while holding down a modifier key.
*   Typing immediately while text remains inadvertently selected.

**Evidence / Observation**

Data loss incidents in text editors frequently trace back to accidental selection overwrites followed by panicking keystrokes that break the undo history chain.

**Research Notes**

*   Highlights importance of robust multi-level undo buffers and visual feedback for bulk deletions.
