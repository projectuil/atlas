Unlike buttons with visible labels, keyboard shortcuts lack visual feedforward. Users cannot preview what an unfamiliar key combination will do before executing it, leading to fear of triggering unwanted or destructive actions.

**Human Impact**

*   Hesitation to experiment with unknown shortcuts.
*   Anxiety when pressing keys in mission-critical applications.
*   Dependency on mouse clicks where visual labels provide clear feedback.

**Context**

Commonly occurs when:

*   Working with un-saved document drafts or live databases.
*   Exploring advanced features in enterprise software.

**Evidence / Observation**

User interviews reveal that fear of "breaking something" or "losing progress" is a primary reason non-expert users avoid testing unknown keyboard hotkeys.

**Research Notes**

*   Establishes necessity for interaction feedforward (previewing action results prior to trigger).
