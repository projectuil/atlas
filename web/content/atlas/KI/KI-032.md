High key density and narrow margins around critical operational keys (e.g., Caps Lock next to A, Power/Delete next to Backspace, Touchpad disable keys) cause accidental activation during rapid input.

**Human Impact**

*   Abrupt state disruptions (e.g., ALL CAPS TEXT, sleep mode, page jumps).
*   Loss of concentration while reverting unintended system actions.
*   Frustration with tightly packed hardware layouts.

**Context**

Commonly occurs when:

*   Typing rapidly on compact 13-inch laptop keyboards.
*   Reaching for Backspace and accidentally hitting Power or Insert.

**Evidence / Observation**

Keypress error logs show high rates of accidental Caps Lock toggles immediately preceding or following an A or Shift keystroke.

**Research Notes**

*   Supports design of physical key isolation and software delay thresholds for destructive keys.
