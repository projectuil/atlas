Files related to a single project are often distributed across multiple folders, storage locations, or services. As a result, users must manually gather documents, images, notes, and supporting resources before they can begin or continue their work.

**Human Impact**

*   Increased preparation time.
*   Interrupted workflow.
*   Higher cognitive load.
*   Reduced project efficiency.
*   Greater dependence on memory.

**Context**

Commonly occurs in:

*   Software development.
*   Research projects.
*   Academic work.
*   Business documentation.
*   Collaborative environments.

**Evidence / Observation**

This behaviour is consistently observed when projects span multiple storage systems or applications. Users frequently know that the required resources exist but spend considerable time collecting them from different locations before meaningful work can begin.

**Research Notes**

*   Contributes to **PAT-001 — Context Loss Over Time**.
*   Highlights fragmentation of project resources.
*   Related to project organization and workflow continuity.
