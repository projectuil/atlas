Users frequently acquire new information without immediately understanding how it changes their existing knowledge. They often struggle to determine whether the new information reinforces, modifies, contradicts, or completely replaces what they previously believed. As a result, users maintain outdated mental models or delay integrating new knowledge into their overall understanding.

**Human Impact**

*   Slower learning.
*   Increased cognitive load.
*   Inconsistent understanding.
*   Delayed knowledge adaptation.
*   Reduced decision quality.

**Context**

**Commonly occurs in:**

*   Academic learning.
*   Professional development.
*   Cybersecurity.
*   Scientific research.
*   Business strategy.

**Evidence / Observation**

This behaviour is consistently observed as users continuously acquire new knowledge throughout learning and professional work. Although individual pieces of information are understood, users often struggle to recognize their broader impact on previously established understanding. Existing learning systems primarily present new information but provide limited support for showing how it should modify existing mental models.

**Research Notes**

*   Focuses on integrating new knowledge into existing understanding.
*   Highlights continuous knowledge evolution rather than knowledge acquisition.
*   Related to learning, cognitive adaptation, and mental model development.
