As projects evolve, users often encounter files whose relationship to their current work is unclear. Although the files may have been important previously, users struggle to determine whether they remain relevant or can safely be ignored without affecting ongoing tasks.

**Human Impact**

*   Time lost evaluating files.
*   Increased cognitive load.
*   Interrupted workflow.
*   Hesitation during decision-making.
*   Reduced productivity.

**Context**

Commonly occurs in:

*   Long-term projects.
*   Software development.
*   Research documentation.
*   Academic repositories.
*   Business records.

**Evidence / Observation**

This behaviour is frequently observed when users revisit mature projects containing numerous historical files. Users often inspect multiple documents before deciding whether they still contribute to the current objective because traditional file systems provide little indication of a file's present-day relevance.

**Research Notes**

*   Contributes to **PAT-001 — Context Loss Over Time.**
*   Focuses on present relevance rather than deletion or inactivity.
*   Related to long-term project evolution.
