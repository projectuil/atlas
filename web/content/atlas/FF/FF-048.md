As file collections grow, users often maintain multiple documents containing overlapping or closely related information. Although consolidating these files could improve organization and reduce duplication, users frequently fail to recognize when consolidation would be beneficial.

**Human Impact**

*   Duplicate information.
*   Increased maintenance effort.
*   Knowledge fragmentation.
*   Higher cognitive load.
*   Reduced organizational efficiency.

**Context**

Commonly occurs in:

*   Research repositories.
*   Business documentation.
*   Academic work.
*   Software documentation.
*   Personal knowledge collections.

**Evidence / Observation**

This behaviour is frequently observed in mature projects containing numerous documents with overlapping topics. Users continue maintaining separate files because the relationships between them are not immediately obvious, resulting in fragmented knowledge that could otherwise be unified.

**Research Notes**

*   Contributes to **PAT-004 — Storage Without Understanding.**
*   Focuses on consolidation opportunities rather than duplication itself.
*   Related to long-term knowledge organization.
