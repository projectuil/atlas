Information within a project often originates in one file, is refined in another, summarized in a third, and presented in additional documents. Users frequently struggle to understand how information moves between these files, making it difficult to trace the origin, transformation, and final use of project knowledge.

**Human Impact**

*   Increased analysis time.
*   Higher cognitive load.
*   Difficulty validating information.
*   Reduced project transparency.
*   Slower decision-making.

**Context**

Commonly occurs in:

*   Research projects.
*   Software documentation.
*   Business reporting.
*   Academic research.
*   Collaborative environments.

**Evidence / Observation**

This behaviour is consistently observed in projects where information evolves across multiple documents. Users often identify individual files but cannot easily trace how ideas, data, or decisions progressed throughout the project. Existing file systems organize documents independently without representing the flow of information between them.

**Research Notes**

*   Contributes to **PAT-003 — Relationship Blindness.**
*   Highlights information flow rather than simple file relationships.
*   Related to traceability, knowledge organization, and project evolution.
