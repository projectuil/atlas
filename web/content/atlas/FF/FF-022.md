Users often encounter multiple files with similar names or similar content but cannot remember why each file was created. Although the files appear related, their individual purpose or role within a project becomes unclear over time, making it difficult to choose the appropriate file.

**Human Impact**

*   Time lost comparing similar files.
*   Increased confusion.
*   Higher cognitive effort.
*   Risk of selecting the wrong file.
*   Reduced productivity.

**Context**

Commonly occurs in:

*   Research documentation.
*   Software development.
*   Business reports.
*   Academic assignments.
*   Projects with multiple drafts or supporting documents.

**Evidence / Observation**

This behaviour is commonly observed in long-term projects where multiple documents evolve over time. Users often recognize that several files are related but cannot remember the specific purpose of each one, requiring them to open and compare files before continuing their work.

**Research Notes**

*   Contributes to **PAT-001 — Context Loss Over Time**.
*   Differs from version management by focusing on remembering the purpose of similar files rather than identifying the latest version.
*   Related to contextual understanding and project organization.
