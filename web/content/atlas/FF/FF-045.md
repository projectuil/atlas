Users often hesitate before editing a file because they are uncertain whether it is safe to modify. They may not know whether other users are actively relying on the file, whether it serves as an official reference, or whether changes could unintentionally affect ongoing work.

**Human Impact**

*   Delayed work.
*   Increased uncertainty.
*   Higher cognitive load.
*   Fear of introducing errors.
*   Reduced productivity.

**Context**

Commonly occurs in:

*   Shared documents.
*   Software repositories.
*   Research projects.
*   Business documentation.
*   Collaborative environments.

**Evidence / Observation**

This behaviour is frequently observed in environments where files are shared among multiple users. Even when users have permission to edit a file, they often postpone making changes because they cannot confidently assess the impact of those modifications on collaborators or dependent workflows.

**Research Notes**

*   Contributes to **PAT-003 — Relationship Blindness.**
*   Focuses on modification safety rather than technical permissions.
*   Related to collaboration, governance, and workflow integrity.
