Before beginning or resuming a task, users often gather files from multiple folders, storage locations, or applications. Even after collecting several resources, users remain uncertain whether every required file has been found. This uncertainty frequently leads to repeated searching and interruptions during the task.

**Human Impact**

*   Increased preparation time.
*   Interrupted workflow.
*   Repeated searches.
*   Higher cognitive load.
*   Reduced confidence before starting work.

**Context**

Commonly occurs in:

*   Research projects.
*   Software development.
*   Business reporting.
*   Academic assignments.
*   Collaborative workspaces.

**Evidence / Observation**

This behaviour is consistently observed when tasks depend on multiple supporting resources. Users often collect documents, images, spreadsheets, notes, and reference materials before beginning work but still question whether an important file has been overlooked. Existing file systems provide limited support for verifying the completeness of task-specific resource collections.

**Research Notes**

*   Contributes to **PAT-003 — Relationship Blindness.**
*   Focuses on completeness of resource collection rather than locating individual files.
*   Related to project preparation and information organization.
