Users frequently develop beliefs based on previous knowledge and experiences. When new evidence challenges these beliefs, they often struggle to revise their understanding, even when the evidence is credible. Existing mental models influence how new information is interpreted, making it difficult to replace outdated conclusions with more accurate ones.

**Human Impact**

*   Slower learning.
*   Resistance to change.
*   Reduced decision quality.
*   Increased cognitive conflict.
*   Delayed adaptation.

**Context**

**Commonly occurs in:**

*   Scientific research.
*   Professional training.
*   Cybersecurity.
*   Business decision-making.
*   Everyday learning.

**Evidence / Observation**

This behaviour is consistently observed when users encounter evidence that conflicts with their existing understanding. Rather than immediately updating their beliefs, users often reinterpret, question, or delay accepting the new information because it challenges established mental models. Existing learning systems provide access to updated knowledge but offer limited support for helping users revise previously held beliefs.

**Research Notes**

*   Focuses on updating beliefs in response to new evidence.
*   Highlights the challenge of changing existing mental models.
*   Related to learning, adaptation, and cognitive flexibility.
