Users frequently encounter files whose purpose is unclear. Although the files are accessible, their filenames, location, or metadata provide insufficient information for users to understand why they exist or how they relate to ongoing work, requiring additional effort to determine their relevance.

**Human Impact**

*   Time lost inspecting unfamiliar files.
*   Increased cognitive effort.
*   Interrupted workflow.
*   Reduced confidence during file navigation.
*   Slower project understanding.

**Context**

Commonly occurs in:

*   Shared folders.
*   Legacy projects.
*   Downloaded resources.
*   Team collaboration.
*   Long-term personal file collections.

**Evidence / Observation**

This behaviour is commonly observed when users revisit older projects or access files created by other people. They often need to open multiple files before determining whether a document is useful, obsolete, or unrelated to their current task.

**Research Notes**

*   Contributes to **PAT-001 — Context Loss Over Time**.
*   Focuses on understanding a file's purpose rather than locating it.
*   Related to knowledge organization and information discovery.
