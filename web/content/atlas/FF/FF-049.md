Users can usually evaluate individual files but often struggle to assess the overall condition of an entire file collection. They cannot easily determine whether the collection is well organized, contains excessive duplication, requires cleanup, or has become difficult to maintain, making long-term management increasingly challenging.

**Human Impact**

*   Poor long-term organization.
*   Increased maintenance effort.
*   Reduced confidence.
*   Higher cognitive load.
*   Declining productivity.

**Context**

Commonly occurs in:

*   Personal file collections.
*   Research repositories.
*   Business documentation.
*   Software projects.
*   Cloud storage services.

**Evidence / Observation**

This behaviour is consistently observed as digital collections continue to grow. Users frequently recognize isolated organizational problems but lack a broader understanding of the collection's overall condition. Existing file systems primarily expose individual files rather than providing insight into the health of the collection as a whole.

**Research Notes**

*   Contributes to **PAT-005 — Complexity Growth.**
*   Highlights collection-level understanding.
*   Related to long-term information governance.
