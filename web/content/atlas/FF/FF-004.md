As software projects grow, navigating their files, folders, and components becomes increasingly difficult. Users often struggle to understand where functionality is located, how files relate to one another, and where modifications should be made.

**Human Impact**

*   Increased time locating project components.
*   Reduced development efficiency.
*   Higher cognitive load.
*   More navigation errors.
*   Slower onboarding to existing projects.

**Context**

**Commonly occurs in:**

*   Large software repositories.
*   Enterprise applications.
*   Open-source projects.
*   Team development environments.
*   Long-term maintenance projects.

**Evidence / Observation**

This behaviour is repeatedly observed as software projects expand in size and complexity. Even experienced developers require significant time to understand unfamiliar project structures, particularly when multiple modules, directories, and architectural layers are involved.

**Research Notes**

*   Contributes to PAT-001 — Context Loss Over Time.
*   Project complexity increases faster than human understanding.
*   Related to navigation, structure comprehension, and information discovery.
