Users frequently make decisions throughout their personal and professional lives but struggle to systematically learn from the outcomes of those decisions. Although successes and failures provide valuable experience, users often fail to connect previous outcomes with future decision-making. As a result, similar mistakes are repeated, successful strategies remain undocumented, and long-term decision quality improves more slowly than experience alone would suggest.

**Human Impact**

*   Repeated mistakes.
*   Slower improvement.
*   Reduced decision quality.
*   Increased cognitive effort.
*   Loss of valuable experience.

**Context**

**Commonly occurs in:**

*   Business leadership.
*   Cybersecurity operations.
*   Software development.
*   Personal decision-making.
*   Project management.

**Evidence / Observation**

This behaviour is consistently observed across long-term personal and professional activities. Users accumulate experience through repeated decisions but often lack structured ways to reflect upon outcomes and incorporate those lessons into future reasoning. Existing information and productivity systems preserve records of activities but provide limited support for transforming past decision outcomes into continuously improving decision practices.
