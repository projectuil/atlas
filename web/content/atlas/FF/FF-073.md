Users frequently face situations where decisions must be made under time constraints. Acting quickly may reduce decision quality, while seeking greater accuracy often requires additional time and information. Users struggle to determine the appropriate balance between making timely decisions and achieving sufficient accuracy, resulting in either rushed decisions or unnecessary delays.

**Human Impact**

*   Delayed decision-making.
*   Reduced decision quality.
*   Increased cognitive load.
*   Time pressure.
*   Decision fatigue.

**Context**

**Commonly occurs in:**

*   Cybersecurity incident response.
*   Emergency management.
*   Business operations.
*   Healthcare.
*   Project management.

**Evidence / Observation**

This behaviour is consistently observed in environments where both speed and accuracy are important. Users often recognize the trade-off but struggle to determine when additional analysis no longer justifies the delay. Existing decision-support systems provide information for decision-making but offer limited guidance for balancing timeliness with decision quality.

**Research Notes**

*   Focuses on balancing decision speed with decision accuracy.
*   Highlights trade-offs under time constraints.
*   Related to decision optimization, uncertainty, and risk management.
