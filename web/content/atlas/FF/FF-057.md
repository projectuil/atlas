Information is often presented with implicit assumptions that remain unstated. Users frequently understand the visible conclusions but struggle to recognize the underlying assumptions upon which those conclusions depend. As a result, users may accept information without evaluating whether its assumptions apply to their own situation or context.

**Human Impact**

*   Reduced critical thinking.
*   Increased risk of incorrect conclusions.
*   Higher cognitive load.
*   Misapplication of knowledge.
*   Reduced decision confidence.

**Context**

**Commonly occurs in:**

*   Academic research.
*   Business strategy.
*   Policy analysis.
*   Technical documentation.
*   Artificial intelligence outputs.

**Evidence / Observation**

This behaviour is consistently observed when users interpret explanations, recommendations, or analytical conclusions. While explicit information is generally accessible, the assumptions supporting that information are often omitted or taken for granted. Existing information systems provide limited support for exposing these underlying assumptions, requiring users to infer them independently.

**Research Notes**

*   Focuses on recognizing implicit assumptions behind information.
*   Extends information evaluation beyond facts and sources.
*   Related to reasoning, critical analysis, and knowledge interpretation.
