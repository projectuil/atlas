Users often struggle to locate files immediately after downloading them. Although the download is successful, they may not remember the save location, filename, or application used. This causes unnecessary searching and interrupts the user's workflow.

**Human Impact**

*   Time lost searching for downloaded files.
*   Interrupted workflow.
*   Increased cognitive load.
*   Frustration and uncertainty.
*   Duplicate downloads.

**Context**

Commonly occurs while downloading:

*   Documents (PDFs, certificates, invoices)
*   Images and media
*   ZIP archives
*   Software installers
*   Email attachments

**Evidence / Observation**

This behaviour has been repeatedly observed across Windows, macOS, Linux, Android, and modern web browsers. Users frequently ask questions such as "Where did my download go?" or "Did it actually download?" The problem becomes more common when multiple files are downloaded or when different applications use different default download locations.

**Research Notes**

*   Contributes to **PAT-001 — Context Loss Over Time**.
*   Affects both novice and experienced users.
*   Observed across desktop and mobile platforms.
