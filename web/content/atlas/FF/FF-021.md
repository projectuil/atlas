Users often accumulate large numbers of files over time but cannot easily determine which files are no longer actively used. Without clear indicators of long-term inactivity or relevance, users retain unnecessary files, making storage increasingly cluttered and navigation more difficult.

**Human Impact**

*   Increased storage clutter.
*   More time spent browsing files.
*   Higher cognitive load.
*   Reduced confidence during cleanup.
*   Lower overall organization efficiency.

**Context**

Commonly occurs in:

*   Personal document collections.
*   Long-term projects.
*   Academic materials.
*   Business records.
*   Cloud storage services.

**Evidence / Observation**

This behaviour is frequently observed as digital file collections grow. Users often recognize that many files are likely obsolete but hesitate to remove them because they cannot confidently determine whether the files are still relevant or might be needed in the future.

**Research Notes**

*   Contributes to **PAT-001 — Context Loss Over Time**.
*   Distinct from file deletion uncertainty by focusing on identifying inactive files rather than deciding whether to delete them.
*   Related to long-term storage management.
