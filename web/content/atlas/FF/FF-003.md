Users often forget the meaning or purpose of file and folder names over time. Names that were once meaningful become difficult to interpret, making it harder to identify the correct file without opening multiple files or folders.

**Human Impact**

*   Time lost identifying files.
*   Increased cognitive load.
*   Confusion during navigation.
*   Reduced confidence when managing projects.
*   Slower task completion.

**Context**

**Commonly occurs in:**

*   Long-term projects.
*   Personal document collections.
*   Software development projects.
*   Academic and research work.
*   Shared folders.

**Evidence / Observation**

This behaviour is commonly observed after days, weeks, or months have passed since a file was created. Users frequently encounter filenames that no longer provide enough context to understand their purpose, especially when similar naming conventions are used across many files.

**Research Notes**

*   Contributes to PAT-001 — Context Loss Over Time.
*   The challenge is remembering the context behind a filename, not reading the filename itself.
*   Often occurs alongside project growth and information accumulation.
