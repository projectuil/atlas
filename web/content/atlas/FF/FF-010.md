Users often need to open several files before locating the information they need. File names, previews, or folder structures frequently provide insufficient context, forcing users to inspect multiple documents to identify the correct one.

**Human Impact**

*   Time lost opening unnecessary files.
*   Interrupted workflow.
*   Increased cognitive effort.
*   Reduced search efficiency.
*   Frustration during information retrieval.

**Context**

**Commonly occurs when searching through:**

*   Research documents.
*   Meeting notes.
*   Project reports.
*   Academic materials.
*   Business records.

**Evidence / Observation**

This behaviour is consistently observed in both personal and professional environments. Users commonly rely on trial and error by opening multiple documents because filenames alone do not clearly communicate the information contained within each file.

**Research Notes**

*   Contributes to PAT-001 — Context Loss Over Time.
*   Highlights the gap between stored information and discoverability.
*   Closely related to semantic search and document previews.
