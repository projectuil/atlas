.


Modern professional tools often assume advanced domain mental models. New developers must master architecture, workflows, and logical reasoning simultaneously before achieving basic tool competence.

**Human Impact**

*   Steep learning curve leading to early frustration and slow onboarding.
*   Overwhelming cognitive load when attempting to perform basic tasks.
*   Dependency on senior developers for simple operations.

**Context**

Commonly occurs during:

*   Onboarding junior engineers onto enterprise codebase environments.
*   Adopting complex developer platforms (e.g., cloud platforms, IDEs, CI/CD tools).

**Evidence / Observation**

New developers often hit execution bottlenecks not due to syntax errors, but due to a lack of mental models regarding underlying system infrastructure.

**Research Notes**

*   Illustrates the gap between tool capabilities and beginner capability levels.
*   Emphasizes the need for progressive onboarding and guided discovery paths.
