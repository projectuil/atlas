.


Extended study blocks without adequate breaks exhaust working memory, leading to steep drops in comprehension, retention, and processing speed.

**Human Impact**

*   Diminishing retention performance over the course of a session.
*   Restlessness, physical fatigue, and mental exhaustion.
*   Disillusionment with learning performance.

**Context**

Commonly occurs during:

*   Marathon study sessions before exams or project releases.
*   Unbroken long-format lecture videos or webinars.

**Evidence / Observation**

Comprehension tests show severe drops in detail retention for content presented after 45–60 minutes of uninterrupted instruction.

**Research Notes**

*   Supports micro-learning structures and active interval management (e.g., Pomodoro technique).
