Commonly occurs when:

*   Relying heavily on rote flashcard applications (Anki) without updating the card complexity.
*   Practicing physical mechanics or instrumental music without altering tempo or style.

**Evidence / Observation**

A learner practices a piano piece 100 times until it is flawlessly memorized, but if asked to start from the middle of the song or transpose it, they completely fail because they only learned a sequential motor pattern, not the musical structure.

**Research Notes**

Explores the concept of "deliberate practice" requiring constant adjustment of the challenge threshold to maintain the zone of proximal development.
