.


Without external feedback or structured diagnostics, independent learners are unaware of what they do not know, leaving crucial blind spots unaddressed until task failures occur.

**Human Impact**

*   Invisible structural weaknesses in skill foundation.
*   Confusion when failing advanced topics without understanding the underlying cause.
*   Inefficient study focus targeted at familiar areas instead of gaps.

**Context**

Commonly occurs during:

*   Entirely self-directed online learning without mentors or graded assessments.
*   Transitioning from structured tutorials to open-ended projects.

**Evidence / Observation**

Self-directed learners frequently practice skills they are already comfortable with while skipping unrevealed weak spots.

**Research Notes**

*   Demonstrates the value of diagnostic assessments and peer feedback loops.
