.


Without explicit competency metrics, self-directed learners either prematurely advance without adequate foundation or become stuck endlessly over-studying basics.

**Human Impact**

*   Perpetual cycle of perfectionism preventing advancement.
*   Prematurely jumping into complex topics leading to failure.
*   Self-doubt regarding readiness to tackle real-world challenges.

**Context**

Commonly occurs during:

*   Self-guided study paths lacking standardized benchmark evaluations.
*   Deciding when to transition from tutorial exercises to personal portfolio projects.

**Evidence / Observation**

Self-taught developers routinely report trapped states ("tutorial hell") where they re-watch introductory courses out of fear they haven't mastered fundamentals enough to move forward.

**Research Notes**

*   Demonstrates the benefit of clear skill rubric benchmarks for self-evaluation.
