.


Educational resources frequently lack standardized difficulty ratings or inaccurate target audience labels, causing learners to select materials that are too trivial or far too advanced.

**Human Impact**

*   Boredom from overly simplistic materials or discouragement from overly complex ones.
*   Lost time evaluating low-quality or misaligned resources.
*   Fragmented learning trajectories.

**Context**

Commonly occurs when:

*   Searching open web video tutorials, blogs, and self-published books.
*   Selecting electives in self-directed professional development.

**Evidence / Observation**

Learners frequently abandon courses within the first 15% because the content target expectation fails to align with their actual proficiency level.

**Research Notes**

*   Highlights the need for objective pre-assessments and clear course prerequisites.
