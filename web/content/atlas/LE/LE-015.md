Abrupt spikes in material difficulty without instructional warning or scaffolded support catch learners unprepared, shattering momentum.

**Human Impact**

*   Sudden loss of confidence and heightened anxiety.
*   Abrupt wall-hitting leading to course drop-offs.
*   Feeling tricked or misled by initial course pacing.

**Context**

Commonly occurs in:

*   Tutorials transitioning rapidly from basic "Hello World" setups to advanced state management.
*   Curricula jumping from basic principles directly to complex capstone projects.

**Evidence / Observation**

Course analytics frequently reveal drastic drop-off spikes localized precisely around unheralded complexity jumps in module sequences.

**Research Notes**

*   Relates directly to Vygotsky’s Zone of Proximal Development (ZPD) and instructional scaffolding.
