.


Human memory naturally erodes inactive information over time. Without periodic practice or spaced retrieval, technical details deteriorate rapidly.

**Human Impact**

*   Frustration at having to relearn previously mastered topics.
*   Reduced confidence when handling periodic or infrequent tasks.
*   Inefficiency caused by repeated reference-checking for familiar concepts.

**Context**

Commonly occurs in:

*   Edge-case code syntax, CLI commands, or annual configuration tasks.
*   Specialized subjects studied during onboarding but unused in daily roles.

**Evidence / Observation**

Engineers regularly look up basic syntax rules for secondary languages they previously mastered after a few months of non-use.

**Research Notes**

*   Grounded in Ebbinghaus's Forgetting Curve.
*   Supports the usage of spaced-repetition and quick-reference cheat sheets.
