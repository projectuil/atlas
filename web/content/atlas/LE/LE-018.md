Looking back at past exercises or notes without remembered context makes the material feel alien, forcing learners to re-read preceding chapters to understand their own past work.

**Human Impact**

*   High activation energy required to review historical study materials.
*   Disconnection from personal past insights and solutions.
*   Inefficiency when building upon earlier project foundations.

**Context**

Commonly occurs when:

*   Reviewing code written months prior during an earlier course module.
*   Opening past notebook entries without descriptive context cues.

**Evidence / Observation**

Users frequently re-watch entire introductory modules because looking at their own completed code exercises fails to trigger memory without surrounding context.

**Research Notes**

*   Highlights the need for reflective summary generation and context-rich annotation during initial learning.
