.


Rigid, standardized time-based learning tracks assume linear, identical absorption rates across individuals, failing to account for differing study strategies, prior background, or cognitive processing styles.

**Human Impact**

*   Demoralization when comparing personal velocity with peers.
*   Inefficiency caused by using poorly fitted study methodologies.
*   Reduced confidence despite spending substantial time studying.

**Context**

Commonly occurs in:

*   Cohort-based bootcamps and standard classroom setups.
*   Fixed-duration corporate training programs.

**Evidence / Observation**

Two learners dedicated to identical 10-hour study modules display vast variance in practical skill execution based on how actively they engaged with the material.

**Research Notes**

*   Highlights why passive time-tracking is a poor metric for true skill acquisition.
*   Points toward mastery-based rather than time-based progress indicators.
