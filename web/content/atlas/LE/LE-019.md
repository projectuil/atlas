.


Complex skill acquisition involves long plateaus where internal mastery develops implicitly without visible output indicators, making learners feel stagnant.

**Human Impact**

*   False perception of failure or lack of talent.
*   Premature abandonment during skill consolidation phases.
*   Loss of enthusiasm and motivation.

**Context**

Commonly occurs in:

*   Intermediate language learning phases.
*   Transitioning from syntax exercises to complex architectural thinking.

**Evidence / Observation**

Learners report high satisfaction during early basic wins, followed by sharp drops in perceived progress during intermediate integration phases.

**Research Notes**

*   Demonstrates the value of objective formative assessments to highlight micro-growth during long plateaus.
