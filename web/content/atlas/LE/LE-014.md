.


Clean, controlled educational examples mask real-world complexity. Learners struggle to generalize abstract rules to messier, varied practical scenarios.

**Human Impact**

*   Inability to translate academic or course knowledge into job performance.
*   Frustration when real-world edge cases break taught models.
*   Dependency on identical example patterns to accomplish tasks.

**Context**

Commonly occurs in:

*   Moving from textbook coding exercises to legacy enterprise codebases.
*   Applying abstract design principles to ambiguous client briefs.

**Evidence / Observation**

Students who easily score 100% on isolated syntax quizzes frequently freeze when asked to build a real-world feature with messy input data.

**Research Notes**

*   Emphasizes scenario-based learning over idealized abstract examples.
