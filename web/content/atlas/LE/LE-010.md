.


Varying naming conventions, metaphors, or mental models across distinct educational sources create cognitive dissonance, leading learners to think identical concepts are entirely distinct entities.

**Human Impact**

*   Conceptual confusion and false sense of complexity.
*   Effort wasted translating terminology across multiple learning sources.
*   Friction when communicating with peers using alternative jargon.

**Context**

Commonly occurs in:

*   Fast-evolving industries (e.g., software frameworks, AI terminology).
*   Consulting multiple documentation sites or video tutorials side-by-side.

**Evidence / Observation**

Learners treat identical concepts (e.g., "Properties" vs "Attributes" vs "Fields") as distinct structural ideas when switching between documentation sources.

**Research Notes**

*   Demonstrates the necessity of standardized glossaries and cross-reference maps.
