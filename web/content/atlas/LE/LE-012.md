.


Passive reading or watching creates an "illusion of competence" through familiarity recognition. Learners mistake understanding an explanation for true capability to apply the knowledge independently.

**Human Impact**

*   Overconfidence leading to failure during unassisted execution.
*   Failure to prepare adequately for practical applications.
*   Shock when unable to solve problems without reference materials.

**Context**

Commonly occurs when:

*   Relying heavily on video tutorials without interactive practice.
*   Reviewing highlighted notes or solution keys passively.

**Evidence / Observation**

Users routinely feel confident following along with video code-alongs, yet experience immediate blockages when tasked with writing code from scratch.

**Research Notes**

*   Highlights the necessity of active recall and formative testing over passive consumption.
