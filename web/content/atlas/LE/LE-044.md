Reading text or watching a video involves passive recognition. The learner feels a rush of understanding because the logic flows cleanly when presented by an expert. However, generating a solution from scratch requires active recall and synthesis. Learners struggle immensely crossing the chasm from "I understand what you just did" to "I can do it myself on a blank page."

**Human Impact**

*   The "Blank Canvas / Blank IDE" paralysis.
*   Over-reliance on tutorials (Tutorial Hell), feeling unable to code or write without a guide open.
*   Severe disconnect between perceived skill level and actual execution ability.

**Context**

Commonly occurs when:

*   Transitioning from watching high-quality YouTube educational videos to attempting independent side projects.
*   Moving from reading language grammar books to having live conversations.

**Evidence / Observation**

A student watches 10 hours of web development tutorials, nodding along in perfect understanding, but when asked to build a simple webpage without looking at the video, they cannot write the first line of syntax.

**Research Notes**

Underlines the necessity of enforced active-recall loops immediately following passive consumption blocks.
