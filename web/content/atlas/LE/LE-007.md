.


Without explicit comparative anchors, learners store new facts in isolation instead of integrating them into existing mental models, making retrieval and synthesis difficult.

**Human Impact**

*   Fragmented comprehension and shallow retention.
*   Inability to utilize analogies to solve problems faster.
*   Rote memorization replacing meaningful understanding.

**Context**

Commonly occurs during:

*   Learning a second programming language or secondary framework.
*   Cross-disciplinary studies where terminology differs across domains.

**Evidence / Observation**

Learners often treat similar concepts in different contexts as entirely unrelated rules, failing to recognize underlying shared abstractions.

**Research Notes**

*   Connects to constructivist learning theories regarding schema integration.
