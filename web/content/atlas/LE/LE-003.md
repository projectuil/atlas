.


Pausing a study routine breaks habit loops and degrades context memory. Resuming requires high activation energy to recall prior context, leading to procrastination or complete abandonment.

**Human Impact**

*   Feeling overwhelmed by the effort required to re-engage with materials.
*   Guilt over lost time and decayed momentum.
*   Abandonment of long-term educational goals.

**Context**

Commonly occurs when:

*   Extended breaks occur due to life events, work deadlines, or holidays.
*   Self-paced courses lack structured re-entry points or refresher summaries.

**Evidence / Observation**

Course drop-off rates jump sharply following intentional or unintentional study breaks exceeding two weeks.

**Research Notes**

*   Demonstrates the impact of memory decay on learning continuity.
*   Recommends contextual "re-boarding" micro-summaries for returning learners.
