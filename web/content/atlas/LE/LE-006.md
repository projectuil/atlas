.


Beginners lack the overarching domain map needed to identify essential prerequisites, making it difficult to establish an effective starting point or sequence.

**Human Impact**

*   Analysis paralysis over where to begin.
*   Wasted effort learning irrelevant or overly advanced topics out of order.
*   Overwhelming feeling caused by unstructured information landscapes.

**Context**

Commonly occurs when:

*   Self-studying emerging fields lacking standardized academic roadmaps.
*   Transitioning into new technical roles without structured orientation.

**Evidence / Observation**

Beginners frequently spend more time searching "What should I learn first?" forums than actively consuming educational material.

**Research Notes**

*   Highlights the importance of visual dependency trees and guided learning paths.
