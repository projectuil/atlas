The self-taught learner operates in an isolated loop. Without a mentor, peer group, or intelligent grading system, errors are baked into muscle memory. By the time the learner seeks external validation, they have to spend months unlearning the bad habits they independently reinforced due to a lack of micro-feedback.

**Human Impact**

*   Cementing of critical errors early in the learning journey.
*   Anxiety regarding whether work is "actually good" or just "done."
*   Inability to break through plateaus due to unrecognized blind spots.

**Context**

Commonly occurs when:

*   Engaging in self-guided online learning without community access.
*   Practicing subjective skills (art, writing, public speaking) where binary right/wrong answers don't exist.

**Evidence / Observation**

A self-taught guitarist plays for two years using a technically flawed hand position; upon finally taking a professional lesson, they are told they must stop playing entirely to unlearn the posture preventing them from advancing.

**Research Notes**

Highlights a massive market gap for asynchronous, AI-driven, qualitative feedback systems.
