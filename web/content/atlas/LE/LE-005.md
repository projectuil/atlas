.


Complex ideas rely on underlying foundation blocks. When materials introduce advanced topics without verifying foundational concepts, learners hit cognitive walls.

**Human Impact**

*   Extreme mental confusion and feeling unintelligent.
*   Need to abandon main study path to fill unexpected learning gaps.
*   Disengagement due to impenetrable technical jargon.

**Context**

Commonly occurs in:

*   Intermediate/advanced technical tutorials lacking clear prerequisite lists.
*   Self-directed learning in complex fields like machine learning or systems architecture.

**Evidence / Observation**

Learners frequently re-read the same paragraph multiple times without comprehension when an unspoken fundamental concept is missing from their mental model.

**Research Notes**

*   Underlines the necessity of explicit prerequisite mapping in curricula.
*   Directly linked to Cognitive Load Theory.
