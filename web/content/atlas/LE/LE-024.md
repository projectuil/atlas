.


One-size-fits-all instruction ignores personal variance in cognitive processing, forcing learners into trial-and-error adaptation to find personalized study habits that work.

**Human Impact**

*   Wasted time attempting popular study methods that clash with personal cognition.
*   Unnecessary self-blame when popular learning formats fail to deliver results.
*   Long experimental period required to discover optimal personalized strategies.

**Context**

Commonly occurs across:

*   Monolithic educational environments using uniform delivery media (e.g., text-only or video-only).
*   Standardized corporate training programs.

**Evidence / Observation**

Learners exposed to identical curriculum materials independently adapt radically different supplementary habits (e.g., drawing mind maps, writing summaries, building practical builds) to achieve competency.

**Research Notes**

*   Highlights the value of flexible, multi-modal instructional formats.
