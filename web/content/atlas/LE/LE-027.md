Curriculum creators suffer from the "curse of knowledge," unconsciously skipping foundational steps or utilizing jargon they assume is universal. Learners encounter abrupt difficulty spikes when they hit these hidden gaps, leaving them unable to parse the current lesson because they lack an unstated foundational building block.

**Human Impact**

*   Immediate loss of confidence and feelings of inadequacy (Imposter Syndrome).
*   Disruption of the learning flow to hunt for missing foundational concepts.
*   High abandonment rates in self-guided learning platforms.

**Context**

Commonly occurs when:

*   Using tutorials created by domain experts for beginners.
*   Jumping between different educational resources or instructors mid-subject.

**Evidence / Observation**

Learners frequently pause video lectures to spend 30+ minutes researching a single acronym or mathematical operation that the instructor skipped over with the phrase "as we all know."

**Research Notes**

Emphasizes the need for dependency-mapping and prerequisite-checking algorithms in adaptive learning systems.
