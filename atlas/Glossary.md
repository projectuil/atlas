Term	                                    Definition

1. Human Friction	        A recurring obstacle, difficulty, confusion, or inefficiency experienced by people while interacting with digital or physical systems.

2. APID	                    Atlas Problem Identifier. The permanent unique identifier assigned to an accepted Human Friction.

3. Category	                A primary classification representing the domain where a Human Friction belongs.

4. Pattern	                A recurring behavioural or structural relationship identified across multiple Human Frictions.

5. Research Insight	        An evidence-based conclusion derived from one or more Patterns.

6. Observation Level	    The confidence level associated with the evidence supporting a Human Friction.

7. Taxonomy	                The classification framework used to organize ATLAS.

As Project UIL introduces new concepts, the glossary grows with it.
