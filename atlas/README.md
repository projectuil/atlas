# ATLAS

**ATLAS** is a structured research repository for observing, documenting, and understanding recurring Human Frictions experienced during interactions with technology.

Rather than beginning with ideas or solutions, ATLAS begins with evidence. Every observation contributes to a growing body of research that supports the discovery of Patterns, Research Insights, and future Research Papers.

---

# Mission

> **Understand human problems before building products.**

> **Products should be built from problems, not from ideas.**

---

# Research Principle

> **Never invent Human Friction. Always observe it.**

---

# Repository Philosophy

ATLAS is a research-first repository.

It is designed to:

- Observe before proposing solutions.
- Build knowledge from evidence.
- Maintain consistent documentation standards.
- Discover recurring behavioural patterns.
- Create a reusable foundation for future innovation.

---

# Guiding Principles

- Observe before concluding.
- Evidence over assumptions.
- One Human Friction per observation.
- Patterns emerge from multiple observations.
- Research should remain objective, consistent, and reproducible.
